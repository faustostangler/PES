Why do wombats make cubic poops? At first we thought it was because they stack so well. Without the round sides, they won't roll away. That makes it a lot easier to mark your territory. But, there are plenty of other scent marking animals that poop, but none of them make it quite so angular. So, then

researchers thought maybe it had to do with the shape of their digestive tract. But again, no. No square sphincter, no square guts. Instead, scientists now think it's to do with them living in super dry parts of Australia. To deal with the extremely hostile conditions, they have to get as much water as they can from their [music] food. And to do

that, their guts have a special set of alternating muscles, which pinch and pull their guts much like this rubber band. You can see if I grab either end and pinch and pull, [music] it forms this square shape. So, the squareness is a byproduct of this gut pinching.
