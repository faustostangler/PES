# [[Estado-fronteira  Marches]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A geopolitical and civilizational phenomenon characterized by a highly militarized borderland state or march, where social structures, fiscal exemptions, and political legitimacy are organized around permanent warfare and territorial expansion.

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Geopolitical positioning on the volatile boundary between Christian and Islamic civilizations during the Reconquista.
- Efeito: The transition of the Portucalense county from a defensive administrative division to an expansionist sovereign state.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Marcelo Andrade, drawing on comparative historical analysis of medieval borderlands.
