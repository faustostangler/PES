# [[Princeps legibus solutus]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A classical Roman and medieval legal maxim ('the prince is not bound by the laws') denoting original, absolute sovereignty, utilized by Afonso Henriques through the title of princeps to assert independence from Leonese suserainty.

Conceito do direito romano e medieval que denota o governante soberano cuja autoridade é desvinculada das leis terrenas tradicionais e de suseranias feudais intermédias.

- Causa: The search for a legal framework to justify the refusal of vassalage to Emperor Alfonso VII of León and Castile.
- Efeito: The adoption of the title 'princeps' (príncipe) in 1129, signaling that the ruler's authority was primary and not derived from any earthly lord.
