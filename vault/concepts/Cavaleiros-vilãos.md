# [[Cavaleiros-vilãos]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A highly militarized social class of non-noble horsemen (commoners) from the frontier region of Coimbra, whose social mobility and status were directly tied to military efficacy and the capture of spoils during the Reconquista.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Condado Portucalense]]

- [[Estado-fronteira  Marches]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The necessity for active defense and territorial expansion against Islamic forces in the volatile southern frontier zone of Coimbra.
- Efeito: The emergence of a highly mobile, militarized society where social advancement was decoupled from traditional noble lineage and tied to combat success.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Marcelo Andrade, 'A Gênese do Reino de Portugal'
