# [[Semiótica do poder]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
The strategic use of symbolic gestures, legal terminology, and ritual actions (such as self-knighting and adopting sovereign titles) to construct, project, and legitimize political authority and independence.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Afonso Henriques]]
- [[Princeps legibus solutus]]
- [[Tratado de Zamora]]
- [[Milagre de Ourique]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The need to break away from Leonese vassalage and establish sovereign legitimacy without relying on traditional feudal superiors.
- Efeito: The legal and symbolic emancipation of the Portucalense territory, culminating in the recognition of the Portuguese monarchy.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Marcelo Andrade, analyzing the symbolic and legal strategies of Afonso Henriques.

## Redes de Conexão (Cross-Context)
- Precursores: ['Feudal law', 'Roman law concepts of sovereignty']
- Eventos Laterais: ["Roger II of Sicily's self-proclamation as king (1130) using Byzantine, Islamic, and Latin legal symbols."]
- Desdobramentos: ["Systematic replacement of 'comes' with 'princeps' and eventually 'rex', establishing a distinct Portuguese diplomatic chancellery."]
