# [[Reino da Sicília]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A centralized medieval monarchy established in 1130 by the Norman leader Roger II, who united Sicily, Apulia, and Calabria by fusing Byzantine, Islamic, and Latin legal traditions.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Apúlia]]
- [[Calábria]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The exploitation of a papal schism and the integration of diverse regional legal frameworks by Roger II.
- Efeito: The creation of a highly centralized state that successfully rejected the suzerainty of both the Holy Roman and Byzantine Empires.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Presented in the text as a contemporary geopolitical and legal parallel to Dom Afonso Henriques' self-assertion of sovereignty in Portugal.
