# [[Conde D. Henrique de Borgonha]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A French nobleman of the Capetian House of Burgundy who became the Count of Portucale through his marriage to D. Teresa of León, initiating the dynastic line of Portugal.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Dona Teresa de Leão]]
- [[Entre-Douro-e-Minho]]
- [[Guimarães]]
- [[Coimbra]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Deployment of French crusading nobility to Hispania in response to Afonso VI's call for assistance against the Almoravids.
- Efeito: Laid the dynastic foundations of the Portucalense County and introduced French feudal and Cluniac ecclesiastical reforms.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Transnational feudal historiography of 12th-century crusading networks.

## Redes de Conexão (Cross-Context)
- Precursores: ['Capetian expansion', 'Cluniac reform movement in Western Europe']
- Eventos Laterais: ['First Crusade', 'Vernacularization of administration under Ghaznavid Persia']
- Desdobramentos: His death in 1112 triggered the regency crisis that eventually enabled his son to proclaim Portuguese independence.
