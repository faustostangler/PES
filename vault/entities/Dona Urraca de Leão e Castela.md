# [[Dona Urraca de Leão e Castela]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
Queen of Leon and Castile and sister of Dona Teresa, who held traditional suzerainty over the County of Portugal, representing the centralizing imperial authority of the Leonese crown.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Dona Teresa de Leão]]
- [[D. Afonso VII de Leão e Castela]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Her inheritance of the Leonese-Castilian crown as the legitimate heir of King Afonso VI.
- Efeito: Geopolitical tension with her sister Dona Teresa, who sought to elevate the County of Portugal to equal status, challenging Leonese suserainty.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Traditional Iberian political history and dynastic analysis.
