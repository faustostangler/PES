# [[Entre-Douro-e-Minho]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A geohistorical micro-region in northern Portugal bounded by the Douro and Minho rivers, serving as the demographic, economic, and political cradle of the Portucalense County.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Guimarães]]
- [[Coimbra]]
- [[Conde D. Henrique de Borgonha]]
- [[Dona Teresa de Leão]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Geomorphological isolation from the Meseta combined with smallholder agricultural ecology and early Reconquest pressura repopulation.
- Efeito: Fostered a resilient, autonomous local baronial class that successfully broke away from Leonese-Galician dominance.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Braudel-inspired long-durational geohistory of Peninsular fragmentation.

## Redes de Conexão (Cross-Context)

- Desdobramentos: Institutional foundation of the independent Kingdom of Portugal and southern territorial expansion.
