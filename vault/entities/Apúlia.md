# [[Apúlia]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A region in southern Italy that was integrated into the newly proclaimed Kingdom of Sicily in 1130 under the Norman ruler Roger II.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Reino da Sicília]]
- [[Calábria]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Norman military expansion and the political consolidation efforts of Roger II in southern Italy.
- Efeito: The incorporation of the territory into a unified, sovereign Mediterranean kingdom.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Identified in historical records as one of the core territorial components of Roger II's self-proclaimed royal domain.
