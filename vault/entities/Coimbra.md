# [[Coimbra]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A strategic frontier city on the Mondego River that served as a key military base during the Reconquest and later as the primary administrative capital of early Portugal.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Entre-Douro-e-Minho]]

- [[Conde D. Henrique de Borgonha]]
- [[Dona Teresa de Leão]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Frontier location on the Mondego with a strong Mozarabic heritage and military proximity to Al-Andalus.
- Efeito: Provided the administrative and strategic base for D. Afonso Henriques to expand southwards and establish a royal chancery.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Dual-axis thesis of Portucalense development (Northern agrarian baronies vs. Southern frontier military hubs).
