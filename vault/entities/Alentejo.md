# [[Alentejo]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A region in southern Portugal that served as the geographic theater for the Battle of Ourique in 1139, marking a critical point in the Reconquista.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Jesus Cristo]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The southward military expansion of Dom Afonso Henriques' forces into hostile Muslim-held territory.
- Efeito: The hosting of the Battle of Ourique, which became the foundational military and spiritual milestone for Portuguese independence.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Cited in Portuguese historiography as the deep southern frontier where the decisive clash of 1139 occurred.
