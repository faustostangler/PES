# [[Guimarães]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A fortified northern urban center in the Entre-Douro-e-Minho region, traditionally celebrated as the cradle of Portuguese nationality and the political seat of the early Portucalense nobility.

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Strategic position as a defensive comital stronghold and ancestral residence of the Portucalense baronial elite.
- Efeito: Hosted the Battle of São Mamede in 1128, securing the political victory of D. Afonso Henriques over Galician influence.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Nationalist and academic historiography of the Portuguese state genesis.
