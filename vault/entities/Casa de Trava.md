# [[Casa de Trava]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
The most powerful noble lineage of medieval Galicia, which, through Fernão Peres de Trava, allied with Dona Teresa of Portugal, sparking intense resistance from the Portucalense nobility.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Dona Teresa de Leão]]
- [[Santiago de Compostela]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Strategic ambition to expand Galician influence southward and consolidate power across the Minho river.
- Efeito: Alliance with Dona Teresa, triggering a defensive reaction from the barons of Entre-Douro-e-Minho and leading to the Battle of São Mamede.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Social-historical analysis of medieval aristocratic lineages and regional power struggles.
