# [[Cholas]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A powerful South Indian dynasty that, during the 12th century, leveraged Dravidian vernacular languages to structure regional court literatures and Bhakti devotional movements, mirroring European vernaculization processes.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The decentralization of classical Sanskrit authority and the rise of regional linguistic identities in southern India.
- Efeito: The institutionalization of Dravidian languages as vehicles for courtly prestige and popular religious devotion.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Comparative historiographical analysis linking Indian vernaculization with the rise of Galician-Portuguese.
