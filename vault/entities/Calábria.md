# [[Calábria]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A southern Italian region that, along with Sicily and Apulia, was unified under Roger II in 1130 to form the Kingdom of Sicily.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Reino da Sicília]]
- [[Apúlia]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The political and military consolidation of Norman territories in southern Italy by Roger II.
- Efeito: The formation of a unified, sovereign Norman kingdom independent of the Holy Roman and Byzantine Empires.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Documented as part of the tripartite territorial foundation of Roger II's royal proclamation.
