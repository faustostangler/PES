# [[Hoysalas]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
A prominent Southern Indian dynasty of the 12th century that utilized regional Dravidian languages to codify local identity and court literature, serving as a global synchronic parallel to the vernaculization of Galician-Portuguese.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Cholas]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The rise of regional consciousness and the shift from classical Sanskrit to Dravidian vernaculars in southern India.
- Efeito: The structuring of localized court literatures and popular Bhakti devotional movements that consolidated regional dynastic power.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Comparative global history framework analyzing the parallel emergence of regional vernacular identities in the 12th century.
