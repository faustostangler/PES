# [[Jesus Cristo]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
The central figure of Christianity, whose legendary crucified apparition to Dom Afonso Henriques before the Battle of Ourique served as the foundational providencialist myth of the Kingdom of Portugal.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Alentejo]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: The need for divine legitimacy and spiritual independence for the newly emerging Portuguese Kingdom.
- Efeito: The creation of the 'Milagre de Ourique' narrative, establishing a providencialist destiny for the Portuguese Crown.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Described by historians as a theological-literary construction that sacralized the Afonsine dynasty and national identity.
