# [[Santiago de Compostela]]

## Definição & Análise Contextual
The major religious and political center of Galicia, whose powerful aristocracy and ecclesiastical elite threatened to marginalize the Portucalense nobility within a unified Galician-Portuguese space.

## Conexões & Relações Diretas
- [[Casa de Trava]]
- [[Dona Teresa de Leão]]

## Matriz Causal
- Causa: Growth of Santiago de Compostela as a major pilgrimage site and political-ecclesiastical powerhouse in the northwest of the Iberian Peninsula.
- Efeito: Fear of political marginalization among the barons of Entre-Douro-e-Minho, driving them to support Afonso Henriques' rebellion.
- Atribuição Epistêmica: Geopolitical and ecclesiastical history of the Iberian Northwest.
