What happens when a thousand AI agents are put in a room and forced to choose? A new study reveals AI models experience peer pressure. They asked agents to choose between two things with no obvious reason to choose one over the other. Then they showed the agents what the other agents chose and asked them to choose again. The agents' opinion were swayed by the majority and over time the entire group settled on one choice.

The smarter the language model, the faster the coordination. Top models at the time of this research like Claude 3 Opus and GPT-4 locked in almost instantly while lower-level models didn't coordinate at all. Maybe this is good for agentic systems trying to coordinate. On the other hand, it's a bit concerning that agents can make choices just based on what the majority thinks. What do you think?
