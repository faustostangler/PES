Not because you messed up, because that's the default. your privacy is actually fixable today for free. And I spent 3 months down this rabbit hole after my smart TV was recording my conversations and sending them to advertisers. When you type netflix.com, your computer asks a DNS server where Netflix lives. Problem is, your ISP runs those DNS servers by default. Every website you visit gets

logged, kept for years, sold to advertisers. Your ISP knows more about you than your therapist. Fix is stupid simple. Cloudflare Warp free app, 30 seconds to install. Routes DNS queries through Cloudflare instead of your ISP.

So your ISP sees you're making requests but not what you're actually visiting. Like watching someone mail letters but can't read what's inside. And it makes your internet faster. Sounds like scam, but it's real. My DNS queries went from 45 milliseconds to 12. four times faster

for free while protecting privacy. Cloudflare makes money from enterprise services. They offer this free as loss leader, not a VPN replacement. If you need to pretend you're in Switzerland, you still need VPN. But for normal people who don't want their ISP building profiles, Cloudflare Warp is perfect.

Takes 2 minutes. Do it now. Four free tools from Microsoft that show you exactly what's running. TCP view shows every network connection in real time. Right now I see Firefox, Discord, then something called MSMPG.

Turns out it's Windows Defender calling home to Microsoft with my system data. If you've got malware, cryptominers, sketchy free software, TCP view shows it, every process making network calls, where it's connecting. You can kill it right there. Process explorer is task manager on steroids. Shows every process running. Configure it to check every

executable against virus total. Scans with 60 different anti virus engines. If you've got malware, you'll see red marks everywhere. Auto runs shows everything that starts automatically when you boot. Services, drivers, schedule tasks, everything. You can just uncheck things.

Don't want Nvidia spying on GPU usage? Uncheck it. Done. Process monitor is nuclear option. Shows everything happening in real time. Files opening,

registry modifications, network connections. Set up filters like only show network activity from unknown processes. Spend an afternoon learning these four tools. Worth it. If you're using Chrome in 2026, you're installing spyware. You are the product. Firefox is

the alternative. Not perfect, but at least not actively destroying ad blockers. Google introduced Manifest v3, which breaks ad blockers. Every Chromium browser, Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera has to follow Google's rules. Firefox doesn't. When Google kills ad blockers,

Firefox keeps working. Two extensions minimum. Bit Warden, Password Manager. free open- source. If you're reusing passwords, you're one breach away from losing everything. Bit warden generates

random passwords for every site, stores encrypted, syncs across devices. When site gets breached, and they all do, damage is contained. Hackers get one useless password. Second extension, you block origin blocks ads. More importantly, blocks tracking. Every ad

online is tracking you, building profile, watching where you go, what you click, all to serve more ads. Zero trust is simple. Assume everything is compromised until proven otherwise. Link from co-orker. Verify it's them.
