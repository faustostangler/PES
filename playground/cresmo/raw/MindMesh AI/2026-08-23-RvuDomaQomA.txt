  AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning are often used interchangeably, but they are not the same.
  
  In this short, we explain the difference using a simple

   **AI** → Makes machines perform intelligent tasks
   **ML** → Helps machines learn patterns from data
   **DL** → Uses deep neural networks to learn complex patterns
