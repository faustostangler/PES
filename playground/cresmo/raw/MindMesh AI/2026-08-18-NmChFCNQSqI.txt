  What is Artificial Intelligence?
  
  AI is the ability of machines or computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as recognizing images, understanding language, making predictions, and making decisions.
  
   AI in Real Life — Examples
  
  • 📱 Face Recognition & Face Unlock
  • 🎬 YouTube & Netflix Recommendations
  • 📧 Spam Email Detection
  • 🎙️ Voice Assistants
  • 🗺️ Traffic Prediction & Navigation
  • 🚗 Autonomous Driving Systems
  • 💬 AI Chatbots
  
  ###  Types of AI by Capability
  
  **1. Narrow AI / Weak AI**
  AI designed to perform specific tasks.
  **Examples:** Face recognition, chess AI, recommendation systems, spam detection.
  
  **2. Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)**
  A theoretical form of AI that could perform a wide range of intellectual tasks with general-purpose intelligence.
  ⚠️ True AGI has not been achieved yet.
  
  **3. Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)**
  A hypothetical form of AI that would surpass human intelligence across many areas.
  ⚠️ ASI does not currently exist.
  
  ### 🔹 Types of AI by Functionality
  
  **4. Reactive Machines**
  AI systems that respond to current inputs without human-like memory of past experiences.
  **Example:** IBM Deep Blue, the chess-playing system.
  
  **5. Limited Memory**
  AI systems that can use relevant past or recent information to help make decisions.
  **Example:** Many modern autonomous-driving systems.
  
  **6. Theory of Mind**
  A proposed future AI capability involving deeper understanding of human emotions, beliefs, intentions, and perspectives.
  ⚠️ This capability has not been fully achieved.
  
  **7. Self-Aware AI**
  A hypothetical AI that would have awareness of its own existence and internal state.
  ⚠️ Self-aware AI does not currently exist.
  
   Important Note
  
  AI can be classified in different ways. In this video, we cover **AI types based on capability and functionality** so you can build a strong foundation before moving into more advanced AI concepts.
