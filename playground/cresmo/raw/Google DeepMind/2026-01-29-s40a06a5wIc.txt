With Project Genie, you can fine tune your world according to the vision you have in mind. It starts here, when you create a world. First, you can describe your environment. A detailed prompt helps with creating a rich, dynamic world. In the description, it helps to add a diverse set of objects.

Then, you can describe your character. In this case, ours is an animal, but it could also be a person, an object, or a vehicle. Talk about your character in the way that you want them to move throughout the world. You can also choose between first person or third person point of view. But it can be hard to know what the world would look like from just text.

That's when you start sketching. Click “Create Sketch” to see a preview of what your world might look like with Nano Banana Pro. From there, you can modify your sketch with text to get what you want. For example, let's add a small house with a fence in the foreground. And maybe some mountains in the distance.

Much better. Now, I can click “Create World” and explore it. World sketching helps you bring your world to life how you envision it. So you can play any world you can imagine.

Start creating worlds of your own at labs.google/ProjectGenie
