mechanical watches are really intricate really precise really small and yet entirely mechanical that's amazing if you want to make any kind of timekeeping device then the first thing you need is something that oscillates where you know how long each oscillation takes a good example is a grandfather clock you have a pendulum where it is known that a single pendulum swing takes 1 second and you can use it to drive the mechanism but if you tried to wear a pendulum clock on your wrist it wouldn't work because the movement of your hand would cause the pendulum to go all over the place in a mechanical clock the pendulum is replaced with an oscillating Mass on a spring except that this Arrangement still relies on gravity and it's going to swing around so instead you use a coiled spring and a rotating Mass the rotating Mass repeatedly winds up and unwinds coiled spring and it can be incredibly accurate a high-end mechanical watch might only lose as many as 5 seconds per day
