video_description: |
  In this second session of the Data Career Masterclass, Kishawna Peck shared actionable insights on creating a standout data portfolio. She highlighted why a portfolio is critical for aspiring data professionals, especially for those without formal work experience. 
  
  Key points covered included:
  - Portfolio Value: Unlike resumes, which list skills, a portfolio demonstrates your abilities through actual projects. This gives hiring managers a concrete view of your technical and problem-solving skills.
  - Essential Components: Kishawna emphasized the importance of clear documentation, business impact explanations, clean code, and showcasing technical depth. These elements help present a compelling story of your problem-solving journey.
  - Project Selection: She advised against common projects (like the Titanic dataset) and encouraged attendees to focus on unique, real-world data challenges that highlight their interests and expertise.
  - Effective Presentation: Using platforms like GitHub or a personal website can make projects accessible and allow hiring managers to easily evaluate skills. She demonstrated portfolio examples that effectively communicate skills in a structured, organized manner.
