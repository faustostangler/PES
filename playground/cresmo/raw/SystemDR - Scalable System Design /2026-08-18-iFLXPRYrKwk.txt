Here's the actual problem. A consumer chat app only ever has to worry about one user's conversation at a time.

An enterprise chat agent has to juggle thousands of simultaneous conversations across different companies, each with its own security requirements, its own compliance obligations, and its own resource limits. Think Slack, but with AI agents serving multiple organizations at the same time on the same infrastructure. The hard part isn't the chat itself, it's tenant isolation. Company A's conversations have to stay completely separate from company B's, even though they're running through the same servers, the same web socket connections, the same underlying agent. Get that wrong even once, and you've got a data leak between two companies who never agreed to share anything.
