Here's the one picture to hold on to for the rest of this lesson. Every piece of content, image, document, or audio moves through four states in order [music] with a detailed audit trail captured at each transition. Received is the moment an upload enters the gateway. Analyzing is where modality specific processing actually runs. Computer vision for images, OCR for documents, transcription for audio.

>> [music] >> Classified is where results from whichever modality ran feed into a unified risk score. And action taken is the final state. The content gets approved, queued for human review, or quarantined based on that score. The detail at the bottom of this slide is the one to really hold on to. A failed analysis doesn't fall through to action taken by default. It loops back

to analyzing with exponential backoff and retries. >> [music] >> That single design decision solves two problems at once. It prevents system overload during high traffic periods by not hammering retries instantly. And it guarantees no content silently slips through unprocessed just because one analysis attempt failed. Nothing exits this life cycle without landing in a definite classified [music] state. Keep

the state machine in mind. The architecture slide next shows the three security layers that actually run inside the analyzing state. Three layers and every single one runs on every piece of content regardless of which modality it is. Input validation is the cheap check that runs first. File type verification, size limits, [music] basic format checks at the gateway.

This is what stops an obvious attack like an executable disguised as an image file before any expensive processing ever touches it. Content analysis is where the real modality specific work happens. And critically, all modalities process in parallel rather than sequentially. Images get computer vision analysis for inappropriate content plus steganography detection for hidden payloads in pixel data. >> [music] >> Documents get OCR'd and scanned for sensitive patterns like social security numbers and credit card formats.

Audio gets transcribed and analyzed acoustically, not just for words, but for things like emotional stress patterns and [music] potential deepfake signatures. Classification and action is where everything converges. Results from whichever modalities were involved feed into one unified risk score, and that single score drives the outcome. >> [music] >> High-risk content gets quarantined, medium-risk gets flagged for human review, and clean content proceeds directly to your AI agent. The unification matters. A document and an

audio file attached to the same request get judged against the same risk framework, not two disconnected ones.
