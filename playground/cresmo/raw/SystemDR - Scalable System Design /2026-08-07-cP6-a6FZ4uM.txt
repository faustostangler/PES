Developers often struggle with SQL's rigid schema. For rapidly evolving apps, forcing dynamic data into fixed tables complicates modeling and significantly slows development cycles. SQL databases demand data conforms to predefined tables with strict columns. This rigid approach bottlenecks dynamic information, hindering adaptability and speed for new features. To cope, developers store complex data as JSON blobs in columns. This

complicates queries and updates, negating SQL strengths and leading to inefficient database storage. NoSQL databases offer a flexible schema, unlike SQL. This enables data storage without rigid structures, perfectly suiting rapidly changing data requirements and agile development practices. Instead of tables, NoSQL stores data as documents, key-value pairs, or graphs. This aligns well with application models, simplifying development and optimizing data access patterns. This

flexibility allows NoSQL databases to natively store diverse data structures, streamlining operations, and providing a more natural fit for modern, evolving application data requirements. NoSQL's flexible schema accelerates development. Teams iterate faster, adapting models on the fly without complex migrations, boosting overall productivity and time to market. NoSQL databases excel at horizontal scaling for modern workloads. They distribute data across multiple servers with ease, efficiently handling increasing traffic and large, evolving data volumes.
