Three failure modes, and each one maps directly to skipping one of the three layers. First, shared infrastructure without real namespace isolation. If tenant separation only exists as a convention in application code, rather than a structural boundary in storage, a single bug in how a key gets generated can leak one company's conversation straight into another session.
