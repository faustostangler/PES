LDA [music] is the default reflex for topic modeling, but it isn't the right default for every corpus. This table is meant to challenge that reflex. [music] On interpretability, NMF tends to produce the crispest, most stakeholder-friendly topics, with LDA close behind, >> [music] >> and BERTopic's embedding cluster topics somewhat harder to explain in plain language. On short text, [music] tickets, tweets, chat messages, LDA's word co-occurrence assumption start to break down >> [music] >> because there simply isn't enough text per document for reliable statistics. NMF and BERTopic both handle sparse, short [music] documents more gracefully.

On compute cost, NMF is [music] typically cheapest and fastest to converge. BERTopic, relying on transformer embeddings, is the most expensive. [music] Where LDA clearly wins is on documents that genuinely mix several themes. Its probabilistic mixture [music] model is built for exactly that, while NMF tends to assign documents more decisively to fewer topics. The rule [music] of thumb, reach for NMF first on short, high-velocity text where speed and crisp topics matter more than modeling overlap. Reach for [music]

BERTopic when semantic nuance across paraphrases matters more than interpretability. And reach for LDA when your documents are long-form [music] and genuinely multi-themed.
