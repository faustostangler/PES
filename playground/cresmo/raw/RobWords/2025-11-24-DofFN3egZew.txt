This unfathomably beautiful part of the world is named after a sexually transmitted disease. This is La Petite France in Strasbourg, which is a city in France but very close to the German border. In fact, it's changed hands between the two countries several times. And traditionally they've spoken a dialect of German here called Alsatian. Anyway, in the early fifteen hundreds, actually, on this site just behind me, they built a hospital here for French soldiers returning from the wars in Italy.

French soldiers who had all contracted the same horrible condition syphilis. And because the only people that the locals encountered with syphilis were French soldiers, they referred to syphilis as Franzosenkrankheit, which means the French disease. Indeed, the English also called syphilis the French disease. But I think that was just a slight on the French. Anyway, as a result, they referred to the hospice housing these poor syphilitic French soldiers as “Zum Französel”, which means to the little French place that in French became La Petite France.

Then over time, La Petite France went from being the name of the building to the name of the entire area, an area that is now part of a Unesco World Heritage Site and an area that is named after syphilis.
