you're sick. He drives you to the airport at 4:00 a.m. and says, "I'm not like other guys. I just want you to be happy. On the surface, it looks like

saintthood, but beneath the benevolence lies a hidden unilateral agreement that you never signed. The nice guy contract is a transactional form of manipulation where an individual performs acts of kindness not out of genuine altruism, but as a down payment on a specific return, usually sex, romantic affection, or a relationship. This dynamic treats human connection like a vending machine. The manipulator believes that if they insert enough kindness coins, listening, buying dinner, doing favors, then intimacy is owed to them as a guaranteed payout. They view their friendship as a currency they are accumulating to eventually buy your love.

Because this contract is covert, they never explicitly say, "I am doing this so you will sleep with me. You believe you are building a genuine friendship. You accept their kindness at face value, unaware that every favor is being tallied in a ledger you cannot see.
