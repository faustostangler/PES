The Beginning of Infinity. This book by David Deutsch is one of the rare life-changing works that completely reshapes the way you see reality. Once you grasp its ideas, you may never think about knowledge, progress, or even the future in the same way again. At its core, the book argues that progress is limitless. Every major breakthrough in science, philosophy, and society stems from one simple fact.

Problems are solvable. There is no fundamental barrier to knowledge, only unanswered questions waiting to be explored. Deutsch dismantles the idea that we will ever reach a point where everything is known, showing instead that the quest for knowledge is infinite. One of the book's most powerful ideas is that knowledge doesn't merely accumulate. It evolves. Weak

explanations are replaced by stronger ones. And this ongoing process fuels scientific discovery, moral progress, and even political freedom. Deutsch emphasizes that this isn't automatic. Civilizations that embrace open-ended inquiry flourish while those that resist change stagnate. This book forces you to rethink reality. It argues that we are

not passive observers of the universe but active participants shaping its future through our understanding. Whether in physics, democracy or even art, progress is always possible.
