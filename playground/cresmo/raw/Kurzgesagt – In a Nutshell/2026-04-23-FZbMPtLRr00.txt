Would you fast-forward your life with the push of a button? You could skip all the bad and boring stuff, 24 hours to get over a hangover, several days a year of commuting time or three years of an entry-level job. You also wouldn't have to live through difficult exams or periods of burnout, and you could even travel decades into the future to get your hands on new technology and witness ground-breaking discoveries. But the world still keeps running, and every time you fast-forward, you disconnect from what's happening. Your close friends change a lot every seven years, and even faster if you don't maintain them. Habits change too. It took humans only a few short years to start ordering food by scanning strange symbols and doing their best work in their pajamas. Skip far enough ahead, and your job might not even exist. Around 40% of essential skills will have completely changed by 2030, so skipping time and training could leave you unemployed. But you don't need to worry about any of this if you're already very rich. Using your money managed in stocks and shares can mean each skip forwards grows your enormous pile while playing your favourite video game. So the question is…
