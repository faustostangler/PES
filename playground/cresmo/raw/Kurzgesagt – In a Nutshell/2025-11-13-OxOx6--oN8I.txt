What's the most dangerous animal on earth? Coming face to face with a lion or a shark is probably one of the scariest things you can think of, but surprisingly only six people a year die from shark attacks and only 200 are killed by lions. Animals that don't look threatening are far more dangerous. Hippos may look like calm and lazy giants, but every year 500 people would strongly disagree if they still could. And dogs are not always our best friends. They're responsible for around 60,000 deaths every year through transmission of canine rapists. One of the most obviously dangerous animals are snakes. They kill more than 137,000 people per year, but even deadlier, a human who are responsible for around 460,000 homicides per year. Still, they're only in second place, overshadowed by the most dangerous animal of them all, mosquitoes. They transmit malaria, dengue fever and zika as one of several other viruses and parasites that ultimately kill 600,000 humans per year. And all it takes is a tiny bite.
