A gammaray burst could destroy us all. These explosions are the brightest and most powerful in the universe, releasing more energy in a few seconds than our sun will in its entire life. Gammay bursts or GRBs are made when a black hole surrounded by a magnetized disc of gas forms during a supernova or neutron star merger. The magnetic field in this gas gets twisted into a magnetic tornado, following the plasma into a jet where it produces a narrow beam of high energy photons that blast across the universe and can scour entire planets of life. A GRB would be over in seconds and almost unnoticeable as it washes over Earth since the atmosphere absorbs almost all of the energy. But this isn't

good news. The high energy photons break apart the molecules in the ozone layer, exposing the Earth's surface to intense solar UV for years. Plants and animals die. Humans get cancer and a mass extinction devastates life on Earth. Fortunately, there don't seem to be any stars within a few thousand lighty years that would produce a GRB anytime soon, so we're probably safe. But if there was
