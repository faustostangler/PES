We're running out of water. By 2030, we're projected to need 40% more fresh water than Earth can supply. Rain keeps making deposits, but our growing populations, farming, and industry are withdrawing water much faster than it can be replaced. Scientists call this water bankruptcy. 70% of major aquifers are shrinking, and many will never recover. Around 1.8 [music]

worldwide, fixing this would cost less than you'd think, about 1% of global GDP [music] or 29 cents per person a day. And it's a lot cheaper than doing nothing.
