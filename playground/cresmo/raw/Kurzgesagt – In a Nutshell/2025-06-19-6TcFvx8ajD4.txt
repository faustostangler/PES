[Music] Why do all planets orbit the sun in nearly the same plane? Let's go back 4.5 billion years in time when our solar system was nothing but a huge spinning cloud of dust and gas. Probably due to the shock wave of a nearby supernova, parts of the cloud started to collapse. Now more of its mass is located closer to the axis of rotation. That's why the

smaller cloud started to spin faster just like ice skaters that pull in their arms. Over time, the faster and faster spinning cloud became a disc. In the center, the hydrogen was compressed so much that it eventually fused into helium and formed our sun. Over the next tens of millions of years, the sun steadily attracted gas and dust from its surroundings. Gravity started to clump the remaining particles together in the spinning disc. Because of the gravity

from the sun in the center, these particles were not able to escape the almost flat plane of the disc. During the next millions of years, planets were formed by countless collisions of these particles. Since all of this happened within the plane of the spinning disc, our planets all orbit the sun in a nearly flat plane.
