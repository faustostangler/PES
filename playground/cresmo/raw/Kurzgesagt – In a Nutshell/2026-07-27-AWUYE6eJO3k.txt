Can we build a secret base in the Moon's darkest region? Because of a slight tilt in the Moon's axis and its path around the Earth, some craters at the Lunapoles never receive direct sunlight. These are the Moon's permanent shadow regions where it's always pitch black and temperatures are colder than Pluto, perfect for our base. We first need a flat spot of land so our land doesn't fall into the crater. Next we'll need power for our construction machines. The darkness rules out direct solar power and sharp regolith dust makes cables vulnerable. But if we set up a remote power station, we can beam energy into the crater with lasers. Now we start to mine what's hidden inside? Millions of tons of lunar ice. We can separate it into hydrogen and oxygen to make rocket fuel or use it to keep the bases starf refreshed. Great! Now let's level up our base even further without going back to Earth for supplies. Equipping our heavy machinery with combustion engines that burn metals from the Moon itself will let us process Moon dust into concrete to build more structures and mine more craters. Soon we'll have all we need to explore Mars and beyond.
