Who's the world's most average person? It's a close call, but as there are slightly more men than women, the average person is male. He used to be from China, but since its population was overtaken in 2023, he's now from India, though he still speaks Mandarin Chinese, and he just turned 31. But what's he actually like? He's 165cm tall and weighs 62kg with black hair and brown eyes. If we put all the food consumed by humans in one big buffet and divided it evenly, he gets around 3000k a day, with around 40% coming from grains like rice and almost 8% from sugar or around 26 sugar cubes. He earns a little over $22 a day or around $660 a month. He owns a smartphone like 60% of humanity, but no car as only one in five people do. Chances are you both have a lot in common, but the average person is constantly changing. By 2050, due to longer life expectancy and declining birth rates, there'll be 6 years older. By 2100, there'll be 42, and with numbers of women set to narrowly overtake men, they'll probably be a woman at the end of the century.
