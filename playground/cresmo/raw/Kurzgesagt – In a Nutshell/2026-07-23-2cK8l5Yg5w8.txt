Something in your kitchen killed you. Sugar seems innocent at first, but it can be lethal. Around 500 teaspoons will turn you blue as it starves your blood of oxygen, sends your body into violent seizures, and stops you from breathing. But could anyone really consume that much sugar? It could be cinnamon which contains cumurine. The liver can turn this substance into a toxic compound that damages liver cells, but this happens only in very few select victims. What about saffron? The world's most expensive spice also contains saffronol which acts on your brain like a sedative. A low dose will calm you, but high doses can be fatal. Salt would be a lot cheaper. The salt in a single soy sauce bottle could be enough to pull water out of your brain cells, shrinking your brain and leading to severe bleeding. But the deadliest killer in your kitchen is bananas, literally. They emit antimatter, and you only need around 3 nanograms, or 437 trillion bananas emitting antimatter for one year to make a tiny banana grenade. When the antimatter annihilates with the same amount of matter, it releases enough energy to rip a hole in your chest, case closed.
