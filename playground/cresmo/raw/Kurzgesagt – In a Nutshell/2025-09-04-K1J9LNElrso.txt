A solar storm could destroy us all. The sun is a violent ball of churning plasma covered in magnetic storms. Sometimes these storms vomit enormous coronal mass ejections of high energy charged particles out into the solar system. While harmless small storms happen regularly, about once every few thousand years, the sun can spit out a super flare which would rush over the Earth and wreak havoc on the planet. While it wouldn't burn the surface, it would violently shake the Earth's magnetic field and damage our global electric grids. It's hard to overstate just how

much we depend on electricity. Not just lights and computers, but also communication, navigation, healthcare, transportation, and food storage all rely on electricity. Worldwide blackouts would cause trillions of dollars in damage. Millions of lives would be lost, and it could take decades to rebuild damaged infrastructure. Luckily for us, if we see one coming and power things down, we can prevent most of the damage.

But we must be prepared. Soda storms like this are not common, so most of the time we'll be fine. bars.
