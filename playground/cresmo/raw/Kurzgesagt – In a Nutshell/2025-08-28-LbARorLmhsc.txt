Mind upload is real. A lot of your head is already in the cloud. If you stored all the data everyone generates online in a single day on 1 TBTE hard drives, you could line them up all the way from New York City to LA. Currently, data is distributed across different platforms, but with big data and AI tools, more and more can be connected. Companies are developing intelligent software that mimics your personality based on collected data. Your digital personality

could be uploaded and chat with your friends. It could even be transferred to a robot that could look, speak, and move like you. Or if you have their data, a family member or a friend. This could help overcome loneliness or the loss of a loved one. But what about digital immortality? Well, we're still not sure

if we can create a perfect digital copy of a brain. To do that, we'd need a superdetailed scan, including the trillions of neural connections that make you you. And if we did create a digital twin, we'd have to ensure it couldn't be hacked or manipulated. So, would you want a digital afterlife?
