How much to wars really cost? Weapons, tanks and planes alone cost the world almost $3 trillion a year. But rebuilding everything they destroyed adds hundreds of billions more to the bill. And costs can stack up long after the bombs start falling. War destroys the land itself. When fields are filled with land mines that cost up to $1,000 each to remove or drone fiber optic cables, they're left unfarmable. Businesses go bankrupt and investors flee. Meanwhile, government responses trigger inflation that eats up civilian savings. Ten years after a conflict starts, countries and their people end up on average 12% poorer. But some of the greatest costs are carried by the younger generations who make up around 40% of war refugees. Those who stay full years behind as schools are destroyed, those who can leave and this brain drain can make countries billions of dollars poorer. But the costs of war aren't just financial. War makes anxiety and depression around three times more likely. Trauma is literally passed on to kids in their DNA. And worse, countries that go to war once are more likely to go to war again. So war doesn't just destroy the present, it also kills the future.
