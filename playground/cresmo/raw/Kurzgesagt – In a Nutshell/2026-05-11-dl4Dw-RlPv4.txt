Newve cork malaria. Now, let's save your life. Malaria is caused by five different parasites and, depending on the one you've got, you'll receive the best drugs to kill it. Usually it's a combination of artermyssinin which is derived from a common weed and kills the parasites as they snack on your blood cells and a partner drug. If your case isn't complicated and you treat it fast, three days of pills and rest will flush them out. For severe malaria, we need to step things up. You'll need drugs piped into your veins for at least 24 hours and may need a blood transfusion or breathing support. Follow-up treatment lasts for days, but with hospital care, the survival rate for severe malaria can be 80-90%. However, if it goes untreated, death is almost certain and unfortunately, many people don't get treated in time. On top of that, malaria parasites are constantly evolving. Treating patients for too little time or over-reliance on a single drug allows parasites to develop drug-resistant mutations. There are new treatments around the corner based on new drugs, but right now, mixing and matching with what we've got makes it more difficult for parasites to adapt and treating patients for longer ensures more people can survive and recover.
