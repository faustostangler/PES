Losing something we love, or someone who is dear to us, can cause us a lot of pain. To accept the loss and overcome the pain, we often go through five stages of grief. Denial, anger, bargaining, depression, and finally acceptance. Getting to acceptance, however, does not mean it is over. Grief is a complex emotional response to loss, particularly the loss of someone we love or something deeply meaningful — like a job. This process is unique to each of us and is not always linear.

Often we cycle back, revisit stages or experience some entirely new emotions. Sometimes it can feel like we are regressing, but with the support of others and each new day, time will help heal our wounds.
