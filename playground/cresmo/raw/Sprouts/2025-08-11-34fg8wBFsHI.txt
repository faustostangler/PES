Do you think the average German car costs more than $90,000? Before Professor Morris ran his experiment, he separated his students into two groups. Professor Morris found that his students who were anchored by the $90,000 figure estimated the average price to be between $45,000 and $50,000, while those anchored by the $30,000 figure estimated it to be between $35,000 and $40,000. The anchors, regardless of the number, had pulled their judgments in that direction. How did you answer? Were you
