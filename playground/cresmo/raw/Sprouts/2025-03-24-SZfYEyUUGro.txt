the German model is especially crucial for children aged 7 to 36 months who are more vulnerable to the stress from separation anxiety the model's objective is for the child to form a secure attachment with a teacher before the bond with their mother or father is interrupted this enables the new caregiver to act as a safe base during the parents absence the model was developed based on the attachment Theory which argues that a strong bond to one primary care caregiver in our first years of life is critical to a child's development if our bond is strong we become securely attached and we feel safe to explore the world knowing we can always return if our bond is weak we feel insecurely attached and as a result are afraid to leave home and explore a scary looking world
