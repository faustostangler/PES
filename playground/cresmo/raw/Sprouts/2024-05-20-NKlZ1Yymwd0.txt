When living conditions worsen and the economy fails, people try to find reasons for their troubles. This is when political extremists can become popular as they blame certain groups of people and promise that by getting rid of them societies’ problems will go away. Extremists divide people into separate groups based on their different characteristics. This often happens to create an us-vs-them mentality or find someone to blame for social problems. This involves assigning symbols or names to members of the other group.

The goal here is to split the society and make it clear who belongs to who. Extremists now demonize the targeted group and try to fully divide society by demanding that everyone must take a side. Those responsible for the cruelty deny their crimes, destroy evidence, blame the victims, and attempt to rewrite history justifying their actions as necessary for the greater good. This is why in the aftermath of genocide, the criminals must be made accountable and the trauma must be addressed. Then the survivors may heal and society can attempt to reunite.
