[Music] What are your thoughts? Have you ever been a victim of your good deeds or felt the urge to punish someone who tries to be a better human being? Unlike normal forms of penalty which aim to discipline free riders and those who break the rules, antisocial punishment penalizes those who contribute to the welfare of the group. This counterintuitive twist on justice, where heroes are treated like villains, targets the cooperative and altruistic.
