  Stop reading about Linux and start breaking it 🚫
  
  Failure teaches faster than any textbook ever will.
  
  I wasted 6 months reading tutorials and watching videos before I realized the problem—I was a theoretical learner trying to master a practical skill. Every article made sense until I actually needed to troubleshoot a real server.
  
  The breakthrough? I forced myself to break things on purpose. Crash a container. Misconfigure permissions. Delete the wrong directory (in a safe environment). Then fix it without Googling the exact answer first.
  
  That's when Linux finally clicked. The panic of "oh no what did I do" followed by "wait I can figure this out" built more real skills than 100 tutorial videos.
  
  Now I teach the same way—hands-on first, theory second.
