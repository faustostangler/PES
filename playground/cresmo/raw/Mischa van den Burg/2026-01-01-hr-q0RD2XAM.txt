When I started out, I I was actually trying to learn Azure and AWS at the same time because I figured that if I learn both of them, then I have a bigger shot at a job because I could do either Azure jobs or AWS jobs, right? That was my reasoning. Well, I know now that this is completely false. In the beginning, you should pick one, focus on that one, master that one, and then later you can branch out because the concepts translate.
