And separate from just DevOps work, if you are a problem on at work, if you are problematic to work with, if you are if you generate friction inside of a team, you won't get hired, you won't get a good reputation, and you won't get any good references. So, human people skills, social skills is something that you have to actively cultivate. Interviewing is a skill in and of itself. If you show up on an interview and you're all tense and and an anxious and you're not able to speak eloquently about what you do, you're significantly lowering the chances of you landing that job. When someone's going to hire you, they want to see someone who is clear on his own skill set and confident in that he can do it. You don't show that if

you're all anxious and you you clearly don't have much experience with interacting with people, especially talking to people who are on a higher leadership level because that's what you'll be doing as a DevOps engineer quite often too. You'll be trying to convince management that Kubernetes is actually the way to move forward etc. And the final element of the human pillar of the tech framework is networking.
