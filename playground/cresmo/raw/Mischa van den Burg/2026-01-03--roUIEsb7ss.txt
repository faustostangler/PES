The DevOps engineer title has always been a bit of a mess. Some companies expected infrastructure, others wanted CI/CD and Terraform. And sometimes you would be putting out fires in production at 2 a.m. It was a role without boundaries. But now that same underlying

DevOps skill set is being absorbed into roles with clearer focus. Platform engineers own Kubernetes tooling and developer experience. Cloud engineers handle scaling. Infrastructure as code and cost optimization. S SRRES drive uptime, monitoring and production health. And yes, even software engineers

are expected to monitor, deploy, and pipeline their own code. In 2024, the most in- demand role for Kubernetes was software engineer, not DevOps engineer. So, the title may be fading, but [music] the skills behind DevOps are actually more in demand than ever. They've just moved. DevOps isn't dead. It's

dissolving into other roles.
