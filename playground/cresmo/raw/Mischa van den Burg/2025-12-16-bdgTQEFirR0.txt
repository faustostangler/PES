  I broke into DevOps without a CS degree by doing this one thing nobody talks about 
  
  When I was transitioning from nursing to tech, every job wanted 2-3 years of experience. Classic catch-22: can't get experience without a job, can't get a job without experience.
  
  The breakthrough? Open source contributions. Not just code.. documentation, testing, configs, anything that shows you can collaborate and ship work.
  
  Here's what actually matters to hiring managers:
  
  ✅ GitHub activity showing real collaboration
  ⚙️ Pull requests with thoughtful code reviews
  🔧 Documentation improvements that help other users
  
  Result? I had proof of my skills before landing my first DevOps role. Hiring managers could see my work, my communication style, and my ability to contribute to real projects.
