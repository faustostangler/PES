Feature removal can reduce the complexity of the browser codebase and help keep browsers safe and secure by reducing potential attack vectors. But if browsers removed features without Warning, well, that would cause websites to break and make it difficult for developers to plan migration to alternatives. Instead, browser vendors take a systematic approach, providing warnings, supporting resources, and detailed timelines well in advance of feature removal.
