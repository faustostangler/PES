Did you know you can tell your AI coding agent to just... fix your website's Lighthouse errors? The Lighthouse tool you know and love is now integrated straight into Chrome DevTools for agents.

Instead of manually copying and pasting failed audits, you can just prompt your agent to run the checks and then let it jump straight into fixing the surface issues on its own. Missing meta descriptions? Sorted. Bad color contrast? Done.
