It's time for CSSRAP 2025, one of my top feature picks is DOM State Preserving Move. Historically moving an element across the DOM was destructive. If you move the playing figure on iFrame using Insert before, it will reload and lose its state. That's changing Chrome 1.3 because we now have the move before. It works exactly like Insert before, but it keeps the element active as you are moving at across the DOM. This means videos keep playing, iFrame zone reloads, CSS animations keep playing, inputs keep your focus while you are actively moving them across your layer. Go check out CSSRAP on Chrome.dev, CSSRAP 2025 to learn more, and see all of the UI features that Chrome shipped this year.
