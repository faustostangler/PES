Today, I want to explain the basics of how passkeys work and how biometrics work alongside them to provide extra security. But first, what even is a passkey? It's like a password, but better. You can use it to log into your favourite sites and apps just like with a password, but in a safer and easier way. Under the hood, it's a public private key pair. Now, unlike with passwords, you don't need to choose between remembering a passkey or saving it somewhere. It's generated for you and is always unique for every site you use.
