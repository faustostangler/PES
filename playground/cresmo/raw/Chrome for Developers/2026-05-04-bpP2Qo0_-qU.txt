AI agents want to browse your site and you should teach them how. Agents are powerful personal assistants that use reasoning and memory to complete complex tasks for your users. Now, they're navigating a web built entirely for humans. They scrape the screen to simulate clicks, scrolls, and wait for animations. It works, but it burns through valuable time and tokens.

What if you could hand the agent an API to understand your site. Enter web MCP, a proposed web standard that exposes structured data for AI agents, defining your capabilities as tools. Why add wemp to your site. First, it makes AI agent interactions faster. Save the agent from having to discover your site's capabilities by providing a list of available tools.

Second, you have two ways to implement it. With a declarative API, the browser translates your existing HTML forms into tools, or build more complex tools with imperative AI. Give the agent direct access to your site's JavaScript. We're just scratching the surface of the web and we want to hear your ideas.
