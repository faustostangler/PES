One of the primary things that people misunderstand about love and a loving relationship is that you can't have love unless you love yourself first. Absolutely wrong. >> [laughter] >> In fact, a loving relationship can help you to love yourself more. It's very rare that somebody is so healed and so enlightened that they completely love themselves. No, not really. We're all imperfect.

We're all continuing to grow. So, have a relationship whether you're healed, enlightened, or not. It doesn't matter. That's one big misconception.
