I have a little notebook in my back pocket that I take out whenever Julie says the four terrible words, "We need to talk." I whip this out really slowly, and I get my pen out really slowly so I can calm myself down, and I open it up on a clean page, and I say, "I'm listening. Talk to me." The goal of conflict is mutual understanding. It has a purpose, not persuasion, not convincing your partner that you're right and your partner's wrong, but finding a way to be right together.

Then, things can work out really well. That's my big invention.
