Overtime, really relationships end with a whimper, not a bang. A good example is the Sloan study that was done of dual career couples. And they were very surprised to find that in a typical evening the partners spent less than 10% of the evening in the same room. And almost all that conversation was errand talk. Who's going to do what when? Their

relationships devolved into a long to-do list rather than them really continuing play and fun and adventure and romance in the relationship.
