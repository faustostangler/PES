What quietly destroys love is criticism, defensiveness, stonewalling, and contempt. And contempt was the absolute best predictor of relationship demise. Contempt is really murdering something in your partner. Murdering their sense of self, their dignity. These insulting kind of comments, comments that have this air of superiority, the sarcasm, the statement or ridicule, the mockery.

These kinds of criticisms are really like sulfuric acid for love. They really destroy love over time.
