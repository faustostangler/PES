  This silent 8mm film from the 1960s was produced by Walt Disney Productions and sold at Disneyland in Anaheim, California as a souvenir. It offers a glimpse into the magic of the park, highlighting various areas, parades, attractions, and rides. Through visuals, the film promotes everything the amusement park has to offer, from iconic landmarks like the Sleeping Beauty Castle to various thrilling, themed rides.
