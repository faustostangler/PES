The pilot may ask you to help refuel the helicopter. He'll signal you when to begin, but before you do anything, take off your gloves. Gasoline-soaked gloves are a fire hazard. Turn on the fuel pump.

Connect the ground cable to the grounding pin. Squeeze the fuel control lever. It works just like the ones at the gas station. Watch for a backflow of fuel and a stop signal from the pilot. He'll be watching the fuel gauge to make sure that the tank isn't being overfilled.

When he signals you to stop, drain what fuel is left in the nozzle into the tank. Then cap the tank and disconnect the ground cable. Put the hose away and shut off the fuel.
