  Created by the Youth Films division of Gospel Films, and produced by American evangelical leader Rev. Billy Zeoli, "Flip Side" stars Veronica Cartwright, James Victor, Astrid Warner, Don Park and others. Aimed at teens, this dramatic Christian narrative questions the values of modern youth, portraying them as selfish, impulsive, and disconnected from deeper purpose. The story then shifts blame to emotionally absent parents who replace love with money and dismissive gestures and risky behaviors. This leads the characters to many forms of conflict, pain, and tragedy, including drug use, illness and even abortion. A powerful presentation of the gospel message follows, emphasizing Jesus’ resurrection and the hope He provides for those burdened by guilt and confusion. There is longing for a new beginning—“the flip side.” The story concludes with a musical call to decision. The film challenges the viewer to personally respond to Christ’s offer of new life.
