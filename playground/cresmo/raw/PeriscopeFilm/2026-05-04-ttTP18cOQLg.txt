  “The Gold Locket” is an elaborate silent, 1950s amateur film by Ralph McNitt. This is the first half of the movie; the second half which included a trip to India is sadly missing. Also unfortunately, we don't know much about McNitt, but there are many intriguing clues in the film! The film documents McNitt and "Uncle Ben"'s look back at family history dating to the Gold Rush, a search for a treasure map, and a trip to Asia aboard the M/S Fermoor. This trip spans Japan, China, Malaysia, Thailand, Sri Lanka, Hong Kong and Indonesia. Footage includes SF’s Chinatown, Tokyo’s Imperial Hotel, the Mojiko Retro District, Chinese junks and sampans in Victoria Harbor, Adrien-Jean Le Mayeur’s home, Tiger Balm Garden and a number of temples and mosques. (Note: We believe the film dates to around 1950 as it identifies Malaysia as Malaya. M/S Fermoor was scrapped in 1960.)
