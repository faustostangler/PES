  Dating to the late 1920s / early 1930s, this silent film contains two segments, the first showing an aerial trip over New York city and the second showing a tourist view of Florida. The "Aerial Trip Over New York" was probably released by Eastman Kodak, and the "Fun in Florida" segment (1937?) is a Castle Films release.
