  "Not the Giant...Nor the Dwarf" (1972) follows the journey of young heroin addict John Sabora and other residents of Gateway House, a therapeutic community in Chicago where addicts confront their behavior and rebuild their lives through mutual accountability. John, clean for over two years after starting heroin at thirteen, reflects on his transformation as new resident. Jackie Moore enters the program. Jackie initially struggles with denial and withdrawal but is challenged by fellow ex-addicts to face the truth and commit to change. Through emotional confrontations, strict discipline, and communal responsibility, Gateway helps residents mature and learn self-respect. The film also shows the program’s outreach efforts, illustrating both the difficulty and hope of rehabilitation.
