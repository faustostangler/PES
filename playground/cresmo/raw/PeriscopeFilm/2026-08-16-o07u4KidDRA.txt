  This short nature film "Wings Over the North" by Alfred M. Bailey showcases the immense seabird colonies of Labrador and the Gulf of St. Lawrence, featuring Atlantic puffins, rare European cormorants, Atlantic murres, razorbills, and northern gannets. Much of the footage focuses on nesting, feeding, courtship, and defensive behavior, including dramatic views of thousands of gannets occupying cliffs hundreds of feet above the sea. The closing emphasis on laws prohibiting the shooting of birds reflects the growing conservation movement of the period and presents these remote islands as protected refuges for North Atlantic wildlife.
  
  Alfred Marshall Bailey was an American ornithologist who was associated with the Denver Museum of Natural History (now the Denver Museum of Nature and Science) in Colorado for most of his working life.
