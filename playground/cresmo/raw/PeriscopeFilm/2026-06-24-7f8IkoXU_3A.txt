  Dating to September through October 1959, this color, silent home movie was shot by members of the Bray family of Pasadena, California, as they visited France, Germany and Switzerland. The film opens with gray scenes along Switzerland’s Swiss Riviera by Lake Geneva and continues to tour Lausanne, Thun, Fribourg and Gstaad. They cross borders between France and Germany frequently touring France’s Kuhlendorf and Chaumont as well as Germany’s Baden-Baden. The Bray’s home movie provides viewers with intimate views through the Swiss Alps and of various historic sights including Thun Castle, Chateau d’Ouchy, Trinkhalle, Beau-rivage Palace, Chillon Castle and more. (Note: Bray family patriarch Ulric Bray, was the founder of the Bray Oil Co. and invented many oil industry processes and devices.)
