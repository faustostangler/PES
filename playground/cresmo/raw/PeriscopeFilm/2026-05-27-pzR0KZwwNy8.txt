  “Adventures in Guatemala and Yucatan” is a 1950s silent home movie-style travelogue, in color, shot by Joseph F. Temrowski. Temrowski ran a real estate firm and opened the Joseph F. Temrowski Automobiliana Museum in Michigan. In these films, a small group of American tourists sightsee a wonderland of flowers and rugged country, Lake Atitlan, Antigua, Chichicastenano, Chichen-Itza, Yucatan, and Havana, Cuba.
