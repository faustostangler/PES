Developed as a shift from earlier, more literal phrasing like "Pause and refresh yourself," this slogan became one of the brand's most successful, long-running advertising campaigns, emphasizing a relaxing break from the busy, daily grind. At the time that these ads were made, refrigerators were becoming a mainsay of American households, so the ads promote having Coke at home.
