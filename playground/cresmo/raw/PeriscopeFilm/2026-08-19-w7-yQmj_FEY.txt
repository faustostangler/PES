  Dating to 1966, "Glen Canyon" was produced for the Sierra Club from a slide presentation by Phil Pennington. It shows what Glen Canyon looked like before the Glen Canyon Dam was completed in 1963 and the sights flooded under millions of tons of water. Shown here are the spectacular side canyons, ancient Anasazi ruins and other sights now hidden within the depths of Lake Powell.  The film is narrated by Richard Moore and Rolf Russell, written by Phil Pennington and David Brower. It contains photographs by various people including Ansel Adams, Philip Hyde, Bruce Kilgore, Dan Luten, Terry Sumner, Cinde Thomas, Jim Turner, Sam Greene, Eliot Porter, David Gaskill, Gudrun Gaskill, Dick Norgard, and others.  It was produced by Larry Dawson Productions San Francisco, California.
