  This 1963 silent film is the second of two reels titled "Belgium of the Masters". The travelogue showcases the attractions and historical landmarks of Antwerp, Mons, Bastogne, Liège, Namur, and Dinant. The film was photographed by Jan Boon, edited by George Halligan. It was written, directed and would have been narrated live in front of an audience by Russ Potter.
