  Dating to April of 1944, this reel of gun camera footage shows planes of the VIII Fighter Command and the  4th, 78th, 352nd, 355th, 357th, 359th fighter groups in action over Occupied France. This includes air to ground attacks, but the reel also contains footage of dogfights.  During this time period the VIII operated Lockheed P-38 Lightnings, Republic P-47 Thunderbolts, and North American P-51 Mustangs. Note: Some of the German losses on the ground in this period were immense.  The 78th Fighter Group set the record for the European Theater when they destroyed 135 planes on the ground on their mission of April 16th, 1945.
