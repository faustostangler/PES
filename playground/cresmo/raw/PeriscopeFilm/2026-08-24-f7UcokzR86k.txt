One mark of the intoxicated driver is his fixed and determined stare at the road ahead. A normal driver sees a broad scope of roadway and his eyes move to both sides. But the drunk must doggedly keep his full attention on maintaining the vehicle in a relatively straight course. Often, too, the intoxicated driver is forced to close one eye to eliminate the alcoholic curse of double vision. Another mark is his stranglehold on the steering wheel.
