See how smoothly the 1956 Pontiac starts and moves into traffic. Now watch how even as we gain speed, accelerate quickly or slowly, there's no jump or momentary jerk as the power is transmitted to the rear wheels. Nor when we slow down for a stop, is there any appreciable jerk. That's the StratoFlight transmission, easy and smooth with positive controlled engine braking action, too. Power and smoothness for hill climbing, for getaway.

And proof of this StratoFlight smoothness can be seen from this simple comparison. In a car with a manual shift, such as this one, no matter how expert the driver is, gear changes from first to second to third still are jerky. With a standard Hydramatic, the gear changes were much smoother, but you still felt them.
