  Here is a showreel of TV commercials produced by the Doyle, Dane and Bernbach (DDB) advertising agency in the 1970s. The majority of commercials are for Polaroid International promoting cameras (circa 1977) in different countries. The cameras included the Polaroid 1000, 2000, and EE33. Some of these advertisement feature the renowned mime Marcel Marceau, who performs different skits which he enhances with a Polaroid instant camera. The film also features several commercials for Mobil gas stations and their branded expansion into Florida and Georgia. (Note: these commercials were donated to us by Charles Abrams, who was an art director at DDB at the time they were made.)
