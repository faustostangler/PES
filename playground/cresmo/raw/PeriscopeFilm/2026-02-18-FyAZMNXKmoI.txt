  Shot by an unknown filmmaker, this 1970s Super 8mm home movie shows some of the summer activities of a Black / African American family. They include several family gatherings both in their home and on trips to a public park and flower garden in Los Angeles. The family then takes a trip to San Francisco, were they see some sights like the Golden Gate Bridge and Alcatraz Island. The film ends with a close-up of a woman bringing out her baby from a house.
