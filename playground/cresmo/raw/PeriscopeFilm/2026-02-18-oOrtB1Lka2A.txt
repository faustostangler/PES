  Shot by an unknown American amateur filmmaker, this 16mm silent home movie captures intimacy among family intermingled with footage of wreckage from a devastating tornado circa 1952-55. Color shots open with the twin’s graduation; two young girls in dresses and later in graduation caps and gowns pose in the front and backyard. It transitions to scenes of tornado damage including mangled trees, interior guts of homes slumped along residential streets and shots of debris removal crews. A number of twisters caused significant damage in 1955. The film is possibly shot in either Udall, Kansas or Blackwell, Oklahoma. Two F5 tornados wreaked havoc from May 25th- 26th. Footage highlights the destruction a tornado can leave in it’s wake and the simplicity of family time in the 1950’s; pigtailed blondes play in the kiddie pool, kin gathers for a backyard summer party, youth and adults splash in a lake on summer retreat and play jump rope on the driveway in autumn. It’s a blend of universal and personal history.
