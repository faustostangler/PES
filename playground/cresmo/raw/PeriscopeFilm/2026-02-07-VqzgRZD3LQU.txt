  This silent home movie was made in the 1930s by an unknown American amateur filmmaker. It  shows a family at their lake house in the USA and then an epic trip to France, Italy, Germany, and the Netherlands. During the trip to Europe they visit the French Riviera and cities in Northern Italy, including Venice and Como. They then take a ship down the Rhine through Germany to Holland, before returning to the USA from Liverpool. The film ends with a color sequence with extended footage of a family outing on a boat and an overview of the family’s large lakeside house.
