  This untitled, silent, black-and-white home movie chronicles an American family’s trip in Italy and France during the early 1930s. The film includes footage of Venice, Lake Como and Villa d’Este, Nice and the French Riviera as well as the French Alps. While the family is unknown, it appears this film is one of many travelogs made by this couple as they appear in other travelog films through Europe.
