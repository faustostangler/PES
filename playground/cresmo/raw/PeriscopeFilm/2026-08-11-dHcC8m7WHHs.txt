This is the story of tuna, of the smaller, better tasting tuna packed under the StarKist brand. Many of you, like famous Hollywood star Billie Burke, may already be familiar with the tender, light meat in the StarKist cans. But you may not have realized that behind each can of tuna is a dramatic story of the sea. The story of the fishing fleets in the South Pacific where the finest tasting tuna in the world are to be found. This is San Diego Bay.

It is from here and from Los Angeles Harbor that the ships of the StarKist fleet start on their big game hunts. The ships themselves are large, sturdy, and sparklingly clean. Those shown here each have room for more [music] than 300 tons of tuna in their refrigerated holds. They're the pride of the tuna fleet with a cruising range of more than 25,000 mi and the seaworthiness to withstand the battering of the roughest weather. You will see later how important that last factor is.
