  This reel of silent footage focuses on a variety of test programs run out of Eglin, Kirtland, Holloman, Vandenberg and Edwards Air Force Bases in the 1960s. This includes cold weather simulations for aircraft, Project Gemini, simulated weightlessness through parabolic flight, gunships, and the Fulton Surface-to-Air Recovery System (STARS).
