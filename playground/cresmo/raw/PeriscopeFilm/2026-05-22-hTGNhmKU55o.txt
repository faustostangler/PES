  Shot between 1929 and 1932, this silent home movie footage shows scenes at Vassar College in Dutchess County, New York. Founded in 1861 by Matthew Vassar, Vassar was the second degree-granting institution of higher education for women in the USA; it didn't become co-educational until 1969. The film includes footage of the campus grounds and buildings, and shots of students on bicycles, swimming, picnicking, riding horses, dancing, and dressed up to perform a play (4:00). It also includes what might be a May Day celebration (5:00) and graduation ceremony (6:00). 
  
  Motion picture films don't last forever; many have already been lost or destroyed. For almost two decades, we've worked to collect, scan and preserve the world as it was captured on 35mm, 16mm and 8mm movies -- including home movies, industrial films, and other non-fiction.
