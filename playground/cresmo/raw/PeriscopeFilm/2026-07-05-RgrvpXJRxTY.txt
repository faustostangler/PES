  This 1963 silent film is the first of two reels titled "Belgium of the Masters". The travelogue showcases the attractions and historical landmarks of Brussels, Bruges, and Ghent. The film was photographed by Jan Boon, edited by George Halligan, and features maps illustrated by Rand McNally & Co. It was written, directed and would have been narrated live in front of an audience by Russ Potter.
