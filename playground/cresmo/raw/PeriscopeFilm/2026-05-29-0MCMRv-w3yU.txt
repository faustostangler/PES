  This silent, black-and-white home movie from 1957 captures a visit to downtown Los Angeles and the Port of Los Angeles in San Pedro. It showcases some of the city's most prominent political buildings, including Los Angeles City Hall, the California State Building, and the Los Angeles Hall of Justice. The film also features recognizable landmarks and locations throughout LA, such as the San Pedro Harbor, the Municipal Ferry, Union Station, the Biltmore Hotel, and more.
