So, narrow startups are companies that build incredibly opinionated deep products, charge very high prices for a relatively small number of people. The simple math is that charging 41,000 people $200 a month is a 100 million run rate business, and there's a lot of precedent for this already occurring. We see Google Ultra's top SKU is 250 a month, Grok is 300 a month, OpenAI's is 200 a month, I believe Anthropic's is 200 a month. Consumers are flocking to these products organically. They're paying high prices for them and over again we're seeing them delivered the value that they expected. So, the whole

idea behind narrow startups is build small, go deep, and charge a lot.
