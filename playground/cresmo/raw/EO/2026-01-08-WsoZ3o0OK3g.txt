When I was still in high school, I would just like spend my entire summer 2 hours per day just like cold emailing bunch of professors, 900,000 professors from like top 20 universities, Georgia Tech, Caltech, Harvard, Stanford. And I got a lot of rejections, 99.9% were all rejections cuz I'm a high school student, even though I said I would love to work for free. Try to keep it under five four sentences and jump straight to the point. What do you actually want to get from that person?

Give an introduction about yourself, two three sentences. You get straight to the point. Here's the email, Professor Ilya, who actually allowed me to work for him for free. He's the head of venture capital at Stanford. Hello, my name is Arlan. I would really really love to

explore virtual unpaid internship. Here's my resume. He didn't respond and I didn't give up after that. I kept sending follow-up emails for like 2 months I sent three more emails. I said like I wanted to follow up on our previous conversation in case you didn't get my email. The message is like really

really straightforward and that's like when I got an acceptance. If they didn't respond, I would just continue sending emails every single day.
