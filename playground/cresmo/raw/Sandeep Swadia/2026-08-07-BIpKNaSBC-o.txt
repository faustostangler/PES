Every unfinished task in your head is like an uninvited guest who refuses to leave. Every email you opened but didn't reply to, every meeting that ended without a clear next step, they stay open in your brain in the background. Like an app on your phone you forgot to close and it's draining resources. >> [music] >> So, the habit of controlling the input has two steps. First, do your process in batches. [music]

When I was a CEO, I had two or three fixed windows a [music] day, emails and Slack. If something was truly urgent, my team knew how to find me. And second, when you're going through your batch of items, touch each item once and only once. Now you have four options. Do it, or delegate it, or date it, or delete it. Otherwise, that thing will linger on

and on forever.
