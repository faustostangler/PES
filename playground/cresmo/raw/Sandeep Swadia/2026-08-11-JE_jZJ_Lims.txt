The most powerful source of energy ever discovered is inside the smallest thing in nature. In 1945, the Manhattan Project did something that changed the world forever. They took the smallest thing in the nature, the atom, and split it, and the energy released from that single act destroyed two cities. That is our first energy habit, quantize the action. Because the hardest part of any habit is starting it. If you want to write a book, writing the first line is the hardest. If you want to clean up your kitchen, picking up the first dish is the hardest. Quantize your habit, or break the habit down into actions that are the smallest possible units. Writing one line, doing half a push-up, walking for one minute, is all about reducing the force required to start. And once you're moving, physics is on your side.
