Your afternoon coffee has a half-like that quietly disrupts your tomorrow. Caffeine has a half-life of about 5 or 6 hours. That means if you drink a cup of coffee at 4pm, half of that caffeine is still circulating in your system at 9pm. And who knows a quarter of it might still be there after midnight. So you feel like you slept well, but you're not getting the deep rest that you needed. And what's the solution? You reach for another cup of coffee and the cycle feeds itself. So one habit I have started recently is to cut caffeine at least 6 hours before bedtime. Because the most productive thing you can do for your career, your clarity, and your relationships is to protect your sleep. The coffee that can save your afternoon can also sabotage your next morning.
