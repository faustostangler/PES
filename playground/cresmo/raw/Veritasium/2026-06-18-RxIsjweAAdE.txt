This magnetic blob might one day save your life. Why? Cuz doctors right now are struggling to treat brain tumors. They can use chemotherapy, but it's like using a grenade to kill a mosquito. That's why researchers at ETH Zurich have developed this. It's a little

gelatin blob that's wrapped in an iron oxide coating, and it's going to change everything. They inject the blob into your bloodstream, and then using low-strength magnets guide it at speeds up to 80 cm a second right to the tumor. Once it's there, they can then flip the magnetic fields back and forth, which deforms the gelatin ball causing it to heat up, dissolving it, and then releasing the drug so that it can attack the tumor. It sounds like science fiction, but it's real. In trials at the university, they've achieved dosages 7,000 times lower than using chemotherapy.
