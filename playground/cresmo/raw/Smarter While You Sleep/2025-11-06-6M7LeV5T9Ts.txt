Empathy is perhaps the most misunderstood of all human superpowers. It's often confused with sympathy, which is feeling sorry for someone, or with compassion, which is wanting to help alleviate suffering. While these are valuable qualities, empathy goes deeper.

When we observe others experiencing emotions, specialized cells called mirror neurons fire in our brains as if we were having those experiences ourselves.
