You can't unknow what you learned about them, about yourself, about how quickly solid ground can crack beneath you. What rebuilds instead is different. It's smaller at first. More careful. Constructed on entirely different ground rules than the original. It has names now. I need you to tell me where you are. Answer by eight. I'm not ready to meet their friends yet. Conditions the old trust didn't require because it had the luxury of blind faith. The new thing is thinner in some ways, thicker in others. You're more honest about what you actually need, less willing to pretend things are fine when they aren't. Some people can't live in that smaller, realer space. They want to be believed the way they were before, without conditions or checkpoints or lingering doubt. They confuse your refusal to pretend with your refusal to try, but they're missing it. You're not refusing to rebuild. You're refusing to rebuild false. The second version might actually be stronger because it's no longer carrying the weight of fantasy. It won't look like the first one. It will look like something that was actually earned. And if they stay long enough to learn the shape of it, they'll realize that's the only version worth having.
