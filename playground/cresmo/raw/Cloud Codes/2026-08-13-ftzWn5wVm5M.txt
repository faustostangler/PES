Nvidia just shipped an open model and it is barely a transformer. Six of its 52 layers run attention. The other 46 do not. Attention is what got expensive. Every token rereads every token before it. So the key value cache grows with

your context. Mamba does not reread. It folds the past into one state that never grows. So why keep any at all? A pure state space model smears fine detail and attention recalls it exactly. Lightning

runs Mamba 2 in 23 layers and spends six on attention. Artificial analysis scored it 824 Elo on GD Pval above Open AI's open weight model and above Nvidia's own Nemotron 3 super, a model four times its size. 3.6 billion parameters fire per token across a 1 million token window. The weights are public and you can run it yourself. Transformers are not dead.

They are outnumbered 6 to 52.
