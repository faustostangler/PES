Hovenweep in southwestern Colorado was the home to six villages with archaeological evidence of human habitation going back a full 10,000 years. Chimney Rock, Walnut Canyon, Hovenweep, Canyon of the Ancients, Canyon de Chelly, and the misnamed Aztec Ruins National Monument are among the many other notable sites which are simply too numerous to list.
