Your glass shower doors are dirty and hard to clean. Let me show you a few tips to make it quick and easy. I go to products for this are either barkeepers friend Moore spray or a DIY mix, which is one cup vinegar, one cup water, and about a teaspoon of dawn dish. Once I've sprayed the glass with a cleaner, then I come through with a non-scrub sponge. This one here is a scrub mommy and scrub in a circular motion. Once the scrubbing's done, I come through and just rinse the entire area with water, and I like to grab a squeegee to remove any excess water from the surface. A quick pro tip is if you squeegee after every shower, the hard water and things won't build up on the glass, making them stay cleaner, longer. To make sure there's no streaks left behind and the glass looks amazing, I always come through with a dry, clean microfiber cloth and give everything a quick buff. But what if you did all these steps and you still have some hard water build up on the glass and it's just not coming clean? For that, I suggest using Steel Wool Grade 0000400. This is gonna give you some extra scrubbing power to remove the really nasty hard water build up. I will say though, you always wanna make sure to spray the surface with the cleaner so it's well lubricated and scrub in a circular motion like you see here. And just like that, these glass shower doors look as good as new and yours can too. Thanks so much for watching and if you're new here, consider following.
