The mistake I see all too often when people are cleaning their houses is that they leave a ton of residue from their cleaners behind and over time that dries sticky and then attracts more dirt, making your home more dirty. When you use a three towel system, you prevent this. The first towel you dip in your cleaning solution and then clean the surface. You're cleaning the cabinets here. We're going to clean it with the cleaning solution towel first. Obviously with this towel, you want to pay special attention to any of those dirty, grimy or sticky areas while you're cleaning because this towel is really doing the heavy lifting and removing that from the surface. Then come through with the second towel, which is just rinsed in some warm water, no cleaner, whatsoever. We're going to rinse the surface that we just cleaned. Once everything is nice and rinsed up, many times people stop here, but they leave a lot of streaks behind in things, especially if it's a more shiny or gloss surface. To level up the clean, the final towel, the third towel, is just a dry, clean microfiber towel that dries and buffs the surface. This will leave not only the surface nice and clean, but your entire house will stay cleaner because you use the three towel system.
