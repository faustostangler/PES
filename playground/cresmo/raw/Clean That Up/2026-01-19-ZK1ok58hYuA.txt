Now I see this all too often when watching other people vacuum, as many times they're going as fast as possible just to get the job done and I get it, not everyone loves this task, but really when you go too fast you don't allow the vacuum time to do its job and suck up all the dirt and things that's in your carpet or on your floors. Now for me I like to recommend just going at a slow and steady pace. This just allows the vacuum time to do its job and suck all the debris out of the carpet. And that way you're really making the most out of your time vacuuming so you don't have to do it again as often. Now the other thing when it comes to going slow is you always want to make sure to kind of overlap by an inch or two each line that you do. That way you're getting good coverage and you're making sure to vacuum up every square inch of the area that you're vacuuming. Now when it comes to working in sections personally I like to work at the furthest point in the room away from the exit and then work my way to the door and depending on the shape of the room I'll kind of work in just like four to five foot sections vacuum that area get it looking great and then move on to the next. If you kind of take a chaotic approach to your vacuuming just kind of do a little bit everywhere sometimes you need to do that in a quick instance but really you miss spots and then you're leaving dirt behind and then your house is not going to be as clean. So when you take time and go slow and work in sections everything gets nice and cleaned up.
