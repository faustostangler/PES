As a front cleaner, I know that safety always comes first when it comes to cleaning your home. That's why I partnered with the American Cleaning Institute to help you keep your laundry room safer during National Safety Month. One of the most important things you can do is keep your liquid laundry packets in their original container with the lid closed after you're done using it. One trend the American Cleaning Institute is encouraging families to rethink is transferring laundry products to decorative containers. In fact, 60% of Americans say they already do plan to or considering storing cleaning products in a decorative container which is a 12% increase from 2024. However, when you keep them in the original packaging, these are designed to help prevent things like mix-ups, misuse, or even accidental exposure to the product. It's also a good idea to remember to put away your laundry products as soon as you get home from the store and make sure they're up high and not a sight. The nice thing about these simple habits is they only take a few seconds but really make your home much safer for you and your family.
