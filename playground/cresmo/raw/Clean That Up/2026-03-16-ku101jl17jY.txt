Vacuum your carpet in all directions. Now, you don't have to do this on every square inch of your room. However, in areas like a high traffic pattern, where you walk a lot, and there's a lot of dirt and debris, or even very shaggy carpet and rugs, this is very important, because what tends to happen in those areas is the carpet fibers will kind of matte down or fall down, and they'll trap dirt and debris underneath. So if you only ever vacuum in one direction, you're not removing as much dirt as possible. Again, you're just cleaning yourself in a circle here, and that way you're getting all directions in all angles of the carpet fiber, nothing's getting trapped underneath, and everything gets nice and clean. Now, I will say, if you've never done this before, you just vacuum in an area in one direction, you're like, yeah, it looks pretty clean. I think it's good. Give this a try. I think you'll be amazed of how much more dirt and hair and everything else that's in your carpet that you pull out.
