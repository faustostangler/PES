Now in my experience of cleaning a ton of kitchen cabinets, there's usually always one that is the most greasy and dirty and you might have guessed it already, but that's the one above your oven and hood range. And I like to suggest using a degreaser. Now the one I like to recommend is just simple dawn dish soap, not the other fancy kind, just the basic blue dawn, because this is going to do a great job to break that down. What I like to do is just add a little bit of dawn to a warmed up scrub mommy and then give that area a really good scrub. Now you don't have to scrub too hard here, just let the dawn do the work of breaking down all the grease and grime. Now once you feel like it's nice and clean, then always come through rinse it really well and make sure to dry the surface. Now you might be wondering why don't we just use dawn to clean all of the cabinets and in my experience repeated use of dawn on wood surfaces has the tendency to kind of dry things out and potentially cause damage and we don't want that. So I really only use it when I need it on these specialty cases and on those areas that have a really bad grease and grime build up.
