If your front-load washing machine rubber gasket is moldy, grimy, or has a stinky odor, let me show you the best way to clean that up. The first place I like to start is to come through and wipe down the entire gasket to get rid of that first layer of debris that's just kind of sitting on the surface. Now when it comes to choosing the right cleaner for this job, I personally prefer to use my DIY vinegar mix. All you need is one cup white vinegar, one cup water, and a horda teaspoon of the blue Dawn dish soap. Mix it up in a spray bottle and just spray down the entire gasket. Now once you've let the cleaner sit for a bit, I like to come through it just a simple toothbrush and give everything a really good scrub. The vinegar is an awesome deodorizer so it's going to help get rid of any of those funky stinky odors. Now the other side of it is it has some Dawn dish soap in there and that's an awesome degreaser because just the laundry detergent and things kind of gets a little scummy and just built up in there as well and you need a degreaser to break through that so you can lift it from the surface and easily clean it up. Now once you're done giving everything a good scrub, what I like to suggest you do is come through with a two-table system to completely rinse and wipe down the entire gasket. Now it's a good idea to always start at the top and just work your way down and pay special attention again to all of those nooks and crannies because you want to make sure that you're not leaving anything behind. When it comes to this job, you might have to clean the surface a few times to completely remove everything.
