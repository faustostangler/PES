Ready started as an open- source project in 2009 and was used for everything from cache and storage through cues, search, analytics, and time series. In early 2024, Reddis announced it was changing its licensing, surprising developers worldwide. A group of Reddis contributors joined forces through the Linux Foundation and for Reddis, creating Valky, a project committed to staying open source. Valky is an open source drop-in replacement for Reddis, requiring no code changes for applications that are moved from Reddius. Tech giants like AWS, Oracle, and Google stood behind it, backing it up and making performance improvements.

For example, multi-threading IO was added to Valky to reach over 1 million requests per second, and Valky 8.1 was just released in April with performance improvements reducing latency, increasing throughput, and lowering memory usage. Customers are loving the responsiveness and performance improvements. Personally, I've changed many projects to use Valky, and I've seen no issues whatsoever.
