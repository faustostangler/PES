You're not going to believe that keyboard. This is the UHK. It splits, lights up, has an LCD, but that's nothing. There are attachable modules that you can switch on the fly. There's a thumb cluster on the left with mouse keys, but on the right, there's a trackball module, a trackpoint option, and my favorite, the world's tiniest touchpad for your thumb. My god, I wish

my beloved Dygma Defy had something even remotely close. The entire board is tinted thanks to the supporting tinting kit, which is perfect on wood, terrible on wool. Like many other boards, even with rubber feet, it slides away. I'm not a big fan of the default staggered key layout, and my typing ability on it is, well, bad. That said, you can configure it, modify keys, and play around. It is, after all, the ultimate

hacking keyboard. I told you.
