Do you think when you go on a negotiation, you should have plan A, B, C, D? >> There's a lot of advice from my Harvard brothers and sisters that you should. One of the real big advantages that I think I have as a former hostage negotiator, you can't tell the bank robber, "I'm leaving." So, it's a psychological crutch. I have no problem
