You don't make a better deal by changing a price. You make a better deal by working on the terms. You pay me $13 an hour. I flip burgers at McDonald's. Maybe I'm not that enthusiastic about that. You pay me $13 an hour. I'm Warren

Buffett's personal assistant. Ooh, I'll do that. In many cases, there's a set of terms that will make the deal more profitable for you without touching the price. And people are emotional over price. I mean, I have cut people's throat over price. And the downstream

consequences I would have made that price not so good.
