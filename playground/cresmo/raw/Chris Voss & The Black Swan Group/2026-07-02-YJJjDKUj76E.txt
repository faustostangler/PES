The commodity is not always money. There're two other commodities at stake in a negotiation. Number one is information, number two is cooperation. I'm in a grocery store the other day. Girlfriend has sent me out to find broccoli sprouts. I don't know what they

look like. I must have passed them 10 times [music] in a produce aisle. I'm looking around, there's a guy stacking produce. Now, that dude is not there to give me directions. If I walk up to him and say, "Hey, man, can you help me find the broccoli [music] sprouts?" He's

going to say, "You know, I don't know where they are. I'm stacking, leave me alone." He knows exactly where they are. So, this is a negotiation. I walk up to him and I say, "You looking for important." And he's sitting and then

[music] he goes, "No." I say to him, "I know I'm just another lazy, stupid customer who's not smart enough to find the broccoli sprouts. Where are [music] they?" Instead of pointing at them, he walks me right over to them. Thank god he did.
