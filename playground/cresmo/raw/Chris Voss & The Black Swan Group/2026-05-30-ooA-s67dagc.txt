The pessimistic caveman survived. The optimistic caveman said, "Yeah, I know that's a saber-tooth tiger, but I'm optimistic that I can make it my pet. I'm going to [music]

walk up to it, try to feed it." And he got eaten, and he didn't have any descendants. Where the pessimistic caveman said, "You know, that looks like a problem. [music] I'm staying away from it." So, if you combine the fact that people are inherently good, but pessimistic, then we get most human behavior.
