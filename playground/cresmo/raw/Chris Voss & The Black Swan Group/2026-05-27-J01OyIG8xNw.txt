In the middle of a business negotiation, I want my words to land. If I've got something that I'm really worried about the way it's going to land, then I'll make it a question. For example, we charge a lot of money for our services, more than you've ever paid. It might seem like we're greedy, and you're going to think about it. And even if I tell you the number, which I will, you'll say to yourself, "Eh, it's probably fair. I can't afford it,

but no, you're not being greedy." Which sets me up for the next conversation.
