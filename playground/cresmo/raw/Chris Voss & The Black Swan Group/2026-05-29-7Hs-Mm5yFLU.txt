How do you negotiate through text? >> First of all, so much of the world has become so digital that if you get with someone and actually have a genuine conversation, they find it refreshing. They're like, "Wow, nobody ever talked with me before. I'm not used to this. This is great." So, the more digital

people are, the more starved they are for human connection. Now, to get back to how do you communicate? Think of it as a chess game. If you were to play chess via text or email, you wouldn't put all your moves in one text. You make a move, they make a move. When they make

a move, you get information. You got to recalibrate every time. So, if you break it down and think of it as chess, then you immediately have an advantage. And then secondly, realize that they ask you a question, the answer is no. You type in and you imagine when you say no, you're saying, "No." They look at that

text and they go, "No." And then it hurts their feelings. So, do what you can to make the tone better.
