Now the next skill I have to use one of my personal favorites, that's not really a skill that's more of just kind of like a tool that you can add that makes Claude a lot more useful, and that is Composio. Now if you're working Claude a lot, you probably end up connecting a lot of your different tools. And when you start adding all of these different tools, it can actually bloat the context quite quickly in Claude and Composio effectively fixes all of those problems. What you do is you just connect Composio to Claude code, and then inside of Composio, you can connect all of the individual tools that you want to use. And any time Claude code needs to use one of those tools rather than having to have them all listed out, and potentially making a mistake or viewing hundreds at the same time, it can just call Composio, search for the correct tool, and then get the response back.
