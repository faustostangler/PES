  Why can you finish a tutorial and still not build anything on your own? It comes down to how your brain actually retains what you learn.
  Studies show passive learning — just watching tutorials or reading blog posts — leaves you absorbing only about 20% of the material. 
  
  Learn actively by writing real code and building things, and retention jumps to as high as 75–90%. That gap is exactly why so many people feel like they're learning but freeze the moment the tutorial ends.
  
  The fix is simple: whatever resource you use to learn to code, it needs to make you write code, not just watch it. If you're not typing, you're not really learning. Put in the hours at the keyboard — that's where the skill is built.
