Copper protects us against too much iron and helps us absorb iron effectively. Copper also helps us transport iron through the body safely. The most dangerous problem with a copper deficiency has to do with what it does to iron. Here's why. Without

enough copper, you get the side effects of iron which are cirrhosis of the liver, increased risk of developing pathogens, the rusting out of the organs, the damaging of your heart, all on top of not being able to use iron in your blood, so you're actually anemic.
