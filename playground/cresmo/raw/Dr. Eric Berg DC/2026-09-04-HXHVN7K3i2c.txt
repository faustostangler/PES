What would happen if you took high doses of vitamin D3 every single day for 14 days? And I'm talking about dosages over 20, 30, 40,000 IUs every single day. Low doses of vitamin D really only focus on your bone and calcium. Higher doses of vitamin D3 taken every day have more of a therapeutic effect on your immune system, on inflammation, and on the rest of the organs in the body. In order for you to create a therapeutic effect of vitamin D3, you need to take higher amounts and to take it on a daily basis.

If you had depression, you'd feel much better. If you had high blood pressure, you would feel much better. If you had low back pain, you would feel much better. If you had inflammation as an autoimmune disease, you would do much better with more vitamin D3. If you're going to take vitamin D3, always make sure you also take it with magnesium, vitamin K2, and zinc.
