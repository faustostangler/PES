Your body is begging for B1 every time you get anxiety, heart palpitations, or a weird inner trembling feeling. Like you're shaking on the inside but not on the outside. It's like a feeling of nervous energy or tension. If that sounds familiar, ask yourself if you also have the following symptoms. Do you wake up at 3:00 a.m. in the middle of

the night and can't get back to sleep? Do your legs feel more restless at night than during the day? Do you tend to get out of breath when you climb up stairs or an incline? Do you ever feel like you just can't get enough air when you're breathing? All of these symptoms relate to a B1 deficiency. Why? Because B1 is

the master switch for the nervous system and without it the nerves can't regulate all of the functions I've just talked about. Not having enough B1 locks you in a state of flight-or-fight mode. One of the best natural forms of B1 is called allithiamine, which is one I recommend unless you have something called peripheral neuropathy, in which case I recommend you take a different type of B1 called benfotiamine because it's a fat-soluble B1 that can penetrate deep into the nerves in your feet to help reduce some of those symptoms.
