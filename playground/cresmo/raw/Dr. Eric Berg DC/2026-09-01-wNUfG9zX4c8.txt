So, if we take high-end extra-virgin olive oil, and we compare it to avocado oil, what's the real difference? High-quality olive oil has very high levels of what's called polyphenols. Polyphenols can act as a powerful anti-inflammatory, which has been shown to create a similar effect in pain reduction to ibuprofen. Whereas avocado oil is very high in certain carotenoids like lutein, which is very good to prevent macular degeneration of your eye. They both can be used for cooking.

I use more avocado when dealing with higher heat. In my salads, I always use a high-quality extra virgin olive oil. My favorite olive oil is called Fattoria Zambianchele e Torri.
