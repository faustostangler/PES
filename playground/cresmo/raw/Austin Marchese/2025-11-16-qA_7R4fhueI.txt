According to World Economic Forum, project managers are among the job categories driving the most net job growth from 2025 to 2030. We're talking about 20 to 25% growth. So, no, the jobs aren't disappearing. But here's what is

scripts, pulling data or automating workflows. A pure manager, on the other hand, they just manage. They delegate everything. They run meetings, they write summaries. This split determines who survives. And I saw this firsthand

at my last tech startup. We had a PM who started using AI agents to handle technical work he couldn't do before.
