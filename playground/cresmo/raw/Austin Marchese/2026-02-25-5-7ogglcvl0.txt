And AI hack that people won't tell you about is the power of notebook LM. So here's how I use it. I rarely read and when I do, I literally forget everything. So I bring in PDFs of books that I really like. Then I upload them to notebook LM. So for example, I have a sales notebook that has all of Alex Ramose's books and all of Russell Bronson. Shout out Bronson, right? From there, I could have notebook LM specific questions and I'll only look at the PDF books I've uploaded. The key here is that AI won't search the internet for both resources. It'll only look at what I care about. Then just make multiple notebooks. Make one for fantasy football. Make one for updating your resume. Make one for real estate. Whatever the fuck you want doesn't matter. This will make it so that the results are from things that you actually trust.
