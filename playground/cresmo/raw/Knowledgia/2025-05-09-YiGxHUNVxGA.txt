How did the Union defeat the Confederacy? First, the North had a larger population, stronger industry, and an extensive railroad network for troop movement. The Anaconda Plan, a naval blockade, strangled southern trade and supply lines. Lincoln's leadership and the emancipation proclamation weakened the South by making slavery a central issue. Militarially, Union generals like Ulysses S. Grant and William T. Sherman led aggressive campaigns, including grants attack on Vicksburg and Sherman's March to the Sea, which devastated southern infrastructure, internal divisions, and declining morale further weakens the Confederacy. After key Union victories and sustained pressure, the South collapsed in April 1865.
