Why wasn’t Ethiopia Colonized? First, the resilience and determination of its people and the effective leadership of Emperor Menelik II, who unified the Ethiopian forces in their war against the Italian invaders. Secondly, the strategic use of European-supplied weapons and the victory at the Battle of Adwa in 1896 were decisive. The Ethiopian victory in the war led to the Treaty of Addis Ababa which recognized Ethiopian sovereignty. Additionally, the Berlin Conference's regulations and Italy's lack of experience in colonial endeavors contributed to the independence of Ethiopia.

Italy would conquer Ethiopia four decades later during the Second Italo-Ethiopian War, but Ethiopia was liberated by the Allies in 1941 and never became a fully established colony.
