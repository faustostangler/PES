Why did the Portuguese Empire collapse? First, after the Napoleonic War, Pedro I, the son of the Portuguese king, declared Brazil independence after Portugal threatened to revoke its political autonomy. Portugal lost its biggest and most important colony. Portugal's empire began to weaken due to competition from industrialized powers like Britain and France. The scramble for Africa gave Angola and Mozambique to Portugal, but the country lacks the resources to fully exploit and defend these colonies. In the 20th century, the Salazar regime tried to maintain the empire, despite growing global decolonization movements. Costly colonial wars broke out in the 1960s across the empire. Goa, Demon and Dio were lost to India in 1961. In 1974, a military coup in Portugal led to the fall of the dictatorship and a rapid decolonization process granting independence to its remaining colonies.
