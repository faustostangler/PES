When did the last Colosseum rule take place? The last known Gladiator fight is believed to have occurred in 404 AD, when a Christian monk, Tilemachus, attempted to stop a fight in the arena. He was reportedly stoned to death by the angry crowd, but his actions deeply impacted Emperor Onorius. Even though gladiatorial games had been a big part of Roman culture for centuries, the emperor issued a ban on gladiatorial combat. However, since some regions may have still hosted fights, Emperor Valentinian III influenced by the Christian Church, reinforced and strengthened the ban in 435 AD to ensure its full enforcement. Even though by the early 5th century, shifting societal values and the spread of Christianity led to widespread condemnation of arena violence, animal hunts continued in the Colosseum. Until 523 AD.
