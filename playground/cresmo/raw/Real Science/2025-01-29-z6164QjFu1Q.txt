the Greenland shark is one of the largest fish in the Arctic Ocean and one of the largest and most mysterious sharks on earth and it would probably suck to be one they live in water as cold as -2° C beneath the Arctic Ice sometimes as deep as 2900 M this is within the bathy pelagic zone or the midnight zone no sunlight penetrates here and the pressure is immense here they EX exist in total darkness nearly completely alone but they're not exactly by themselves they're joined by a repulsive wormlike crustacean parasite that attaches to their eyeballs and this phenomenon is so common for Greenland sharks it's almost a guarantee in one study that looked at over 1500 Greenland sharks from East Greenland scientists found the parasite on 98.9% of them on top of this the molecules within their bodies that allow them to survive the cold temperatures and high pressures of the deep sea make them smell like a big bag of pea and adding even more insult to all of this is that this horrific existence lasts spectacularly long scientists believe Greenland sharks could possibly live to be over 500 years old so the Greenland shark drifts through freezing dark deep water with worms attached to its eyeballs for centuries
