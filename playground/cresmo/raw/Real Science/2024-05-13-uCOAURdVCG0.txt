if you live anywhere near cicas you probably know that they are really loud really really loud in fact if you get close to a tree filled with male cicas the sound levels can reach 100 DB which is approximately the same intensity of noise as a hair dryer forklift or a subway train but the cicas aren't being noisy just to annoy all of their neighbors it's an essential part of their mating and it's different from any other insect crickets and caty dids all produce their chirps by rubbing their back legs together male cicas on the other hand have special organs called timbles that allow them to produce the screeching sound one timble appears on each side of the abdomen and is connected to the timble muscles inside the cicada's body when the muscles rapidly contract in release they pull on the ribbed timbles causing them to click and resonate against the air sacs in their thorax and abdomen
