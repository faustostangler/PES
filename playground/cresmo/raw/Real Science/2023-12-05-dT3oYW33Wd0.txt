whale sharks are the ocean's biggest fish but for a long time scientists could not determine how old they could get that is until they found evidence of nuclear bomb detonations inside their bodies as whale sharks age they gain Stripes along their vertebrae kind of like tree rings but there's been an ongoing debate over how long each growth band takes to form does it take 6 months or 12 getting it wrong could mean over or underestimating whale shark ages by a large margin which has huge implications for their conservation but in 1955 nuclear weapons began being tested by detonating them in the atmosphere these tests doubled the amount of radioactive carbon 14 in the air which eventually landed in the ocean and became absorbed by marine animals these radioactive signatures act like time stamps within the growth bands of whale sharks and now researchers can definitively tell that each band takes about a year to form and knowing that the researchers determined that the giant sharks can live to at least 50 years old
