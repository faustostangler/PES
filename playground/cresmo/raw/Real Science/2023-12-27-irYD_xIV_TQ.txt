This is known as an ant mill, an unsettling phenomenon where army ants march in a spiral, trapped in this creepy, ritualistic behavior until they die from exhaustion. Why do army ants do this spiral of death? Normally, ants follow trails of pheromones left behind by other ants. But, if a lead ant encounters an obstacle, it may have to turn around or find another route, sometimes causing the trail to loop back in on itself. The following ants may get stuck in that loop. And the more ants

that follow the loop, the more the looping pheromone trail gets reinforced. Alternatively, if the following ants lose the pheromone trail altogether, they end up following each other in a rotating, hopeless circle of death. The ants have a hard time breaking out of this loop and often end up dying in mass.
