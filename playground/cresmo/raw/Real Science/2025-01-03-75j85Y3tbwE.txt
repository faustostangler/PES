in March of 1898 construction began on a Railway Bridge over the Savo River in Kenya as part of a massive Railway project linking Uganda with the Indian Ocean several thousand workers camped over an 8 mile or 13 km stretch to build this section of Railway but what started off as run-of-the-mill backbreaking labor soon became an unimaginable nightmare the 9-month period of sheer Terror began when men would crawl into their tents at night to sleep only for their Slumber to be pierced by the screams of someone being dragged into the bush to be devoured it became an almost nightly occurrence the men quickly realized that this was the work of lions two mless male lions to be precise the workers tried to make a shield from the attacks with campfires and Thorn fences but were unsuccessful the Lions could jump over these deterrents months of attempt to kill the Lions failed the Lions outsmarted the people at every turn eventually after many attempted traps bait and night watches the lions were killed their bodies are now displayed at the Field museum in Chicago it's debated how many people were killed before the reign of terror was ended but estimates are up to 135 people
