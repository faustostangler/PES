chimps usually walk quadrupedally using a style of locomotion called knuckle walking this is where they curl their fingers into a fist and put their body weight on their knuckles only the African great apes and oddly some anteaters Pengalin and platypuses do this but why what does this odd group of animals have in common these animals all have highly specialized front feet needed for things other than Locomotion anteaters and Pengalin have extremely long claws that they use for digging up insects that can get in the way of walking platypus fingers have webbing that extend past the fingers to Aid in swimming so knuckle walking helps them to not trip and chimps bonobos and gorillas have long fingers that would become overextended and get in the way of regular walking and those long fingers are a crucial adaptation needed to securely grip branches during brachiation a form of arboreal movement where chimps swing from Branch to branch
