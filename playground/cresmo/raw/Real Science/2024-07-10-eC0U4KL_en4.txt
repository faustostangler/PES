the Egyptian praying mantis lives in the savanas of Africa where it lives on grass doing its best to blend in but here grass can change from brown to Green within a few days following rain however researchers noticed that when the grass was green the mantids were green but when the grass was brown the mantids were Brown as far as we know mantids can't change color like an octopus or chameleon where the color change happens in seconds or minutes triggered by hormones or neurons however they can change color when they mol in this species color change during molting is triggered by the relative humidity around them when the relative humidity is low the nymphs become Brown at the next mol when the relative humidity is high they become green other species of mantids in Africa even turn from green to Black at the end of the dry season when most fires occur a coloration known as fire melanism
