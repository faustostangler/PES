A certain species of fungus specifically targets the periodical cicadas. The spores fill the cicadas' abdomen, rendering them sterile, but they don't kill the cicada. Instead, they seem to hijack their brains, just like the zombie fungus that attacks ants. In males, this modified behavior means they aren't just calling females. They're also doing the female wing flick signals, which draws in other males. And

then the spores get passed along even further in one big creepy cycle. And that's just the start of the weirdness. The fungus also doses the cicadas with psilocybin. Yes, like the psychedelic from magic mushrooms. This tends to happen when the fungal infections are more advanced, causing the cicadas to lose the back half of their bodies entirely, including reproductive organs.

Even so, the drugs make them fly around like crazy and still attempt to mate, even though they no longer have the equipment necessary to do so.
