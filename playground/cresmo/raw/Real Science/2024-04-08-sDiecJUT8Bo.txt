during a solar eclipse animals don't simply act like there's a big cloud passing over they know something is really different and their behaviors sometimes become really strange researchers at the Riverbanks Zoo in South Carolina have collected the following data Western lowland gorillas become aggressive and baboons become anxious and start running around as a group Gibbons display increased vocalizations sound with an atypical vocal structure that scientists only hear from them during an eclipse groups of giraffes Pace nervously and sway lorets huddle together and have increased vocalizations followed by communal silence but perhaps the most interesting Behavior comes from the galopagos tortoise they're normally pretty sedentary but just before totality they started mating and immediately after they all gazed up at the sky in unison so it seems like humans aren't the only ones to have an emotional response to this rare Cosmic event
