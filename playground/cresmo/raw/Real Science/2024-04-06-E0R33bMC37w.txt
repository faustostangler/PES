narwhals belong to the odonto seti or toothed whale order like their distant relatives the sperm whale and the Orca narwals are endowed with teeth but only two of them and in their case the teeth are actually known as tusks female narwhals have two tusks embedded in their jaw which rarely become visible in about 1.5% of females one of those tusks will grow into a huge spear just like the males and in some cases males will have no Tusk though sometimes they'll also grow too for reasons we don't really understand even though the horns are called tusks they have a very similar Anatomy to other mamalian teeth the outer layers of the Tusk are hard material to protect the nerve at the interior the outermost layer is cementum a calcified material to protect the tooth immediately beneath it is the Dentin which is more bone-like and immediately surrounds the pulp tissue
