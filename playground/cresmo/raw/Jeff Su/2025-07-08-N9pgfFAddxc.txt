90% of people pick the wrong chatgbt model and it's sort of open AI's fault. The average user only needs two types of models. First, chat models, GBT- number, and reasoning models. O number. Ignore everything else. Open AAI says chat

models are great for most or everyday tasks like emails. That's misleading. For example, chat models work fine for turning bullet points into a basic email. But if you're dealing with an angry colleague or client, you're going to want to upload that email thread as a PDF and ask a reasoning model for help. The takeaway here is the model you use should depend on the complexity of the task and not the type of task.
