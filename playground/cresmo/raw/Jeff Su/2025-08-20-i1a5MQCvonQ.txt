Everyone says AI can help non-coders build things, but nobody shows you how. Here's something you can try today. I receive this newsletter every week in Gmail, and I want the content saved in a Google doc for easy reference. So, I open up ChachiBt and type act as an automation expert with knowledge of Google app scripts. Create a script that saves emails from the sender to a Google doc. Chachib gives me the exact code and

step-by-step instructions. I just copy the code, go to Google Appcripts, paste it in, and hit run. Now every future instance of this newsletter gets added automatically to this Google document. The best part, Google Appcripts connects to everything Google Workspace. Gmail, calendar, sheets, slides, docs.
