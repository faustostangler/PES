Here are Gemini features that save me hours every week. In Google Docs, I type at AI and instantly get a summary block at the top of my document, which is perfect for long meeting notes. In Drive, I rightclick any folder and hit summarizes folder, and Gemini tells me what's inside without opening 20 files. In the Gemini sidebar, I can reference multiple documents at once. I type at file one, at file 2, and ask it to draft an email using both. On the Gemini web

app, I paste a YouTube link and my presentation and say, "Apply the storytelling framework from this video to my deck for an instant upgrade." On mobile, I use voice of text to tell Gemini, "Add buy groceries to my Google tasks for hands-free task management.
