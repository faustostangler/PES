When it comes to incorporating AI tools into our daily workflows, one of the biggest blockers for every single one of us is the friction of physically typing the prompts into tools like Gemini. For example, we all know by now that AI can improve our writing, but none of us want to type "Assume the role of a senior copywriter with over 20 years of experience" every single time. And this is why TextExpander apps are a complete game-changer. For example, after opening a new chat in Gemini, I would simply type colon colon copywriter, and the entire prompt appears automatically along with the placeholders I've added in beforehand. I personally use the paid version of the Alfred app, but if you're looking for a free alternative, Mac users can use Raycast and install the snippets extension, and Windows users can use something like Beeftext.
