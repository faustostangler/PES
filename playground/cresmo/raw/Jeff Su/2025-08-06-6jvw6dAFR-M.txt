Here's a quick productivity tip for job seekers. Step one, copy and paste all your target job descriptions into a Google doc. Don't worry about formatting. Step two, add the Google doc into chatbt and input this prompt. I pasted a few job descriptions into a Google doc. Your task is to create a

table with the following columns. 1 2 3 4 5 6. And let me download the table in CSV format. I'll leave this prompt in the description for you to copy. After reviewing the output, we can click here to download the CSV file. And now we can

simply upload this file into Google Sheets or Notion for a fully functional database that helps us keep track of applications.
