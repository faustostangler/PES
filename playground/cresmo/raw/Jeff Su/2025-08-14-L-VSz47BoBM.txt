I'm often asked, why use Notebook LM when I can just give my files to Google Gemini? They're both Google AI products, right? Here's the thing most people miss. The word hallucination in AI is just another word for creativity. The more an AI hallucinates, the more creative it gets. The less it

hallucinates, the more accurate it becomes. Notebook LM is built to minimize hallucinations. So, when I need factual answers from my medical report, for example, I would use Notebook LM. The answers are reliable because they're grounded in my sources. But when I'm brainstorming marketing copy or need creative ideas, that's when I switch to Gemini. It takes more creative

liberties, which is exactly what I want. To sum up, use Notebook LM to confirm the facts, then use Gemini to hype them
