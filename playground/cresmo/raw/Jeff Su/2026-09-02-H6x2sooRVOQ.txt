Here's how to give your AI perfect memory, so you never have to correct it again. Step one, turn on the built-in memory in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, and over time, it learns high-level things like your role and writing style. The AI intentionally keeps these high-level, since global memory can influence every chat you have. Put another way, what the AI learns while you help your kid with homework should not carry over into your work chat. Step two, use [music] projects when a bunch of tasks relate to the same area of work. A project keeps related tasks

together, so the AI can create a very detailed memories without unrelated context getting mixed in. For example, if you prefer three bullet points per slide, the AI should apply that to every presentation it creates. Step three, build your own memory system with ChatGPT Work, Claude Co-work, or Gemini Spark. This is the best of both worlds. You get to decide what gets written down, while the AI does the grunt work.

The only downside is that this only works in AI systems like Claude Co-work, Claude Code, ChatGPT Work, Codex, and Gemini Spark. So, there's a bit of a learning curve.
