One of my favorite Google Gemini tips doesn't even take place in the web app. So here I have a Google sheet with a bunch of user feedback. In column E, I can just type equal AI open bracket quotation mark and just start prompting. For example, categorize this feedback under price UIUX privacy or others and reference the corresponding cell. I drag this down, wait a couple of seconds, and Gemini in Google Sheets does its thing.
