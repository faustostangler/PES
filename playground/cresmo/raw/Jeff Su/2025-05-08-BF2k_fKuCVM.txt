AI agents explained in three simple steps. Level one, large language models. You give ChadBt an input, for example, write an email, and it gives you an output. Easy stuff. At this level, the LLM is passive and does not have access to tools. Level two, AI workflows. You

give ChachBT access to a tool like Google Calendar so that the LLM can manage your calendar based on your instructions. At this level, the LLM has access to tools, but is still passive and requires your instructions. Level three, AI agents. Instead of a prompt, you give the AI agent a goal. For example, manage my workplace calendar.

The AI agent first performs reasoning to decide which meetings to accept or reject, then takes action by responding to the invitations. At this level, the AI agent not only has access to tools but is also acting autonomously.
