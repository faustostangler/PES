In education, there's this concept called scaffolding. It's when you use temporary support to learn something that's beyond your current ability. I use it to this day to get better at AI. Let me show you. First, go to claw.ai/artifacts.

These are apps real people built with Claude. Pick any app or artifact that looks interesting. Scroll down and click view full chat. Now you see the entire conversation, every prompt, every revision, every mistake they made. It's like watching over their shoulder as they work. This scaffolding, seeing

exactly how someone else succeeded is a great way to accelerate your own growth. The best part, you don't even need to use Claude. This works in Gemini and ChachiBT. You just need to enable the canvas feature.
