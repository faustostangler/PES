Just because we can use AI for something doesn't mean we should. Case in point, although it's technically possible to create a task from within Google Gemini's web app, it's obviously much faster to just manually add a task, right? That being said, I often find myself using the Gemini mobile app to create tasks, notes, and calendar events with my voice since speaking is faster than typing. The key isn't just using AI, it's knowing when to use what tool. Follow for more.
