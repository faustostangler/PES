By now, you've probably already seen all the cool things Google's latest image generation model can do. But what exactly is the use case? If we're just talking about the final image quality, dedicated apps like MidJourney wins every time. Just compare these two shoe designs. But watch what happens when I take this photo of Michael Jordan Yaoing and ask Nano Banana to replace just MJ's shoes with an existing design. Perfect

integration, same angle, same lighting, which is simply not possible on MidJourney. Bottom line, other dedicated apps are better at producing images.
