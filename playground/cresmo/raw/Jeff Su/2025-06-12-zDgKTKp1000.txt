If you're feeling behind with AI, you might benefit from this. The way to keep up without the overwhelm is to follow the minimum viable toolkit strategy. The idea is with new AI tools and features rolling out every single day, it's impossible to keep up with them all. So, you want to take the complete opposite approach and ask yourself, what is the fewest number of AI tools I can learn to complete 80% of my tasks? If you're a student, for example, the free version of Chajbt or Google Gemini is more than enough. Ignore everything else. If

you're a professional, you might need to add a few more tools to your toolkit. Trust me, as long as you're using AI tools consistently, you're doing better than 99% of your peers.
