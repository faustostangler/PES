Here's an iPhone AI tip 99% of users don't know about. You can long press any text, drag it over to Chat GBT, drop it in a new chat, type out a prompt, and boom, instant analysis. This drag and drop feature works for everything. Images from your photos app, documents from files, and even screenshots. Here's the best part. I go to settings, search

for text replacement, and now I can add a frequently used prompt as a phrase, and assign a shortcut. Now, instead of typing that whole prompt every time, I just typed the keyword and it expands automatically.
