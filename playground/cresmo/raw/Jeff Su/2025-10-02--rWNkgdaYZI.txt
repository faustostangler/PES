So, I think we all know we should be using AI more, but a lot of times AI feels like an extra step when it should be a seamless step. Here's a simple trick to fix that on your phone. This works for iPhone and Android. If you're on iPhone, go to any app with a share button. I just use files. Tap share.

Scroll all the way to the right, tap more, and then tap edit. Toggle on your AI app like chatbt. Drag it next to Airdrop and hit done. If you're on Android, it's even easier. Open any file, tap share, then long press your AI app again to pin it to the top of the system share sheet. Now when you're

browsing articles, you can share them to AI instantly since those apps are now front and center.
