Do the unexpected. Moving your things to the wrong bit can be really nice. It's called syncopation and it's about marking unusual spots in the bit. Unsyncopated. So move it.

But what counts as unusual bit. It's anything that stand out from the pulse of the track. Regular. Make them come late or early melody. We can give it some bounce.

Syncopating can also be to delay an entire phrase as you feel that it's outside the regular pulse, but moving isn't always necessary. You can just emphasize the groovy once. Now check out the everything follows the pulse, but now a new fill. Another cool trick to try out can be to move this entire pattern one 16th note left and you will discover new cool rhythms or get ideas.
