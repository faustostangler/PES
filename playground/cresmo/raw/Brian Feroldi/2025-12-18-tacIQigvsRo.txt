Top Growth ETF Comparison Explained Visually by Brian Feroldi. QQQ is the most popular growth ETF in the world with 400 billion in assets under management. It has very solid long-term returns, although it has a high expense ratio of about 0.2%. VUG is the second most popular growth ETF in the world with 200 billion in assets under management. It also has stellar

long-term returns and it has a far lower expense ratio, which is 0.04%. IWF has 120 billion in total assets under management, has similar great long-term returns, and a higher expense ratio of 0.18%. Then there's SCHD, which is the smallest of these funds, but it has fantastic long-term returns and a rock-bottom expense ratio of 0.04%. All

four of these ETFs deserve consideration in your portfolio. If I had to choose one, I would probably go with SCHD. If visual summaries like this are useful, you know what to do.
