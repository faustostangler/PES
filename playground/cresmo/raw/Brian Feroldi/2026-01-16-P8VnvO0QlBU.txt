10 key lessons from the great book Quality of Earnings. One, always dig beyond the headline numbers. Earnings per share can be manipulated. Take a deeper look to make sure you understand what's going on. Two, cash flow is harder to fake than earnings. Companies

can inflate their earnings reports, but cash flow reveals the true health of a business. Three, keep an eye out for accounting red flags such as frequent write-offs, aggressive revenue recognition, and inventory tricks. Four, inventory accounting can distort results. Keep an eye out for changes in LIFO or FIFO. Non-recurring items are often recurring. If you see the words

extraordinary charges show up, don't ignore them. Six, be aware of overly promotional management. Seven, keep an eye out for frequent changes in accounting policies. All changes should be scrutinized very closely. Eight, always read the footnotes. This is where

the real story and risk are often varied. Nine, compare the company's earnings to its peers. If a company has much higher margins or growth than its competitors, it might be playing games. 10, quality of earnings equals sustainable, repeatable, and transparent.
