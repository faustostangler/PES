Hi, I'm Aki, a minimalist and Matcha artist traveling the world. I often hear friends say, "You're very lucky," or "Miracles happen to you a lot." WHY? Then, my usual response is, "Yeah, I‘m sooo lucky !" haha Today, I want to share the secrets behind that.

In October 2023, during my North America journey, I was super lucky to experience many miracles. So, I’d like to share five ways to make miracles happen to you too . Stay tuned until the end! I've just arrived at Vancouver Airport! From now on, I'm heading to Victoria, where I lived five years ago.

I'm so excited! **1. How to Make Miracles Happen: Lift Your Mood ** One important and straightforward way to make miracles happen, in my opinion, is to lift your mood . Miracles are more likely to happen when you're in a good mood. Think about a time when you were happy.

Everything seemed to go well, right? On the contrary, when you're frustrated, things don't seem to work out. In simple terms, mood × action = result. So that means when your mood is high, even a small action can lead to great results. So, on this trip, I maintained a consistently positive and happy mood, encouraging many miraculous events.

Now, let's explore some of these miracles together! **Unexpected Reunion** At Vancouver ferry terminal, when I got on the bus and found a vacant seat, I noticed the woman sitting next to me vigorously waving her hand. First, I was like “What’s going on? Who are you? ” But I realized she was my language school teacher from five years ago— What a delightful coincidence!

The miraculous encounters continue. The next day in Victoria, while I was waiting for an order at a Cafe, I noticed a tall person walking in. It turned out she is the owner of the shared house I used to live in! We were both surprised! Especially Because, we were actually planning to meet on the night of this day. Wow!

**2. How to Make Miracles Happen: Visualize the Future** One reason for this miraculous encounter, likely lies in the power of visualization. I believe challenging yourself and stepping out of your comfort zone can lead to unexpected results. While things inside the comfort zone may stay within expectations, stepping outside often brings the unimaginable .

By the way, in Calgary, I took on another challenge. I was asked by Steven, the vice-principal of a local high school, "Aki, could you demonstrate tea ceremony for our high school students?" However, as time passed, I honestly began to forget about that dream. But during this trip, while conducting a tea ceremony in Central Park, it suddenly hit me, "Oh, right! Holding a tea ceremony here was a dream of mine." Somehow, without me realizing, the dream had come true. It felt like a miracle, especially considering I wasn't doing YouTube five years ago.
