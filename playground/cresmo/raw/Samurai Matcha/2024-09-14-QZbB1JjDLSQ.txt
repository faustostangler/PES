This is the simplest way to make matcha. Recipe: 2g of matcha, a container, 65°C hot water. Just put 2g of matcha in the container, add hot water, and mix.

I named this the gym method because making it gives me a bit of a work out too. Then you can have delicious matcha! If you want it smoother, you can use a tea strainer.

2g of matcha and dd 65° hot water, 90g and I'm going to use Chasen There are different ways to mix, but I usually go for about 15 seconds. First 3 seconds to blend the matcha and hot water together.

Next 7 seconds, whisk vigorously back and forth mixing in some air. Then, for the last 5 seconds, move the whisk gently over the surface to smooth out the foam.

It’s better to warm up the whisk beforehand to make it more flexible. This is what I usually do, this is kind of a tea ceremony way This makes me so calm and also I can make it flexible and make the tea bowl warmer. So that you can have very delicious matcha! Match better!!!
