Hi everyone, I am a Japanese minimalist who’s traveling North America for over one month with only two bags. This is everything I brought on this trip. In this video, I’ll introduce you to what's inside my bag and how I packed it. As a minimalist, I’ll also give you some recommendations. So let’s enjoy the stressless trip together!

Clothing How much clothing should we bring for a long trip? I think many people have this question. The answer would be four days worth of clothing, as they are pretty bulky. That means we need to wash them once every four days, which I personally don’t think is too much work. All right, then, let me introduce what I brought.

Kimono

This kimono is like my best friend. It was made at the local kimono store in Japan. Since it’s very sturdy, I’m wearing it everywhere I go these days. Obi (SOUSOU) This obi is highly recommended since it’s a belt for kimono that you can simply tie. Very easy to use.

Puffer jacket It’s perfect for use as a coat during travel since you can make it very small. Gown（gift） Hoodie (UNIQLO) Heat-tech tops x 2 (UNIQLO) White T-shirts x 4 (UNIQLO) Pants x 3 cotton pants (TOMMY) Airism pants (UNIQLO) hiking pants (FJALLRAVEN WAY) I mostly wear some whitish colors for the tops and some darker colors for the bottoms, so that will match with anything you choose. Shorts x 3 Momohiki/Long Johns (UNIQLO) gym shorts (YONEX) Swim shorts Underwear x 4 (UNIQLO) I always use reddish underwear from Uniqlo. Heat-tech bottom (UNIQLO) Socks ×5 4 black socks and 1 white socks for tea ceremony Shoes x 2 Running shoes (On) Sandals (Montbell) I wasn’t sure which shoes to bring, but it’s actually more than enough to bring pairs of sandals and running shoes because how light you can travel matters a lot. “Is this enough?”

Yes, I know you might have thought so. But this is just enough even for both hot and cold days. For example, this is how I wear: For hot days: Day 1. T-shirts A, Long Johns, sandals Day 2. T-shirts B, gym pants, sandals Day 3. T-shirts C, Airism pants, sandals

Cold days: Day 1. Heat-tech tops A, T-shirts D, puffer jacket, cotton pants, socks A, running shoes (On) Day 2. T-shirts A, hoodies, puffer jacket, cotton pants, socks B, running shoes (On) Day 3. T-shirts B, hoodies, puffer jacket, heat-tech bottom, hiking pants, socks C, running shoes (On) +(picnic sheets) Day 4. Heat-tech tops B, hoodies, kimono, Airism pants, socks D, running shoes (On) (+ picnic sheets) How do I pack them?

So I roll them all up and fit them into the mesh travel cases (MUJI). The tip here is to separate the colors of mesh cases. So depending on the color of the case, I categorize underwear, pants, T-shirts, and coats. So that I can find them easily. I like rolling up all the clothes so I can take them out easily too.
