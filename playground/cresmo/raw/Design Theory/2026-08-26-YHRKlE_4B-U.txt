This is a De Tomaso Pantera. It was bought for $2,500 in 1976, but just 2 years later, the car's value skyrocketed to an astonishing $300,000. It was a terribly unreliable car, suffered a lot of wear and tear, and most intriguing of all were the bullet holes that pierced the steering wheel. How does a used car with bullet holes in it sell for more than 100 times its original price? It all comes down to scarcity. Elvis Presley, the king of

rock and roll, was in a heated argument with his girlfriend, Linda [music] Thompson. When Linda left, Elvis stormed off and jumped into the car, probably hoping to dramatically peel out of the parking lot. But instead, the car wouldn't start. So, in a moment of fiery passion, he took his Colt 45 and shot at the steering wheel. Those bullet holes serve as an autograph. There's never

going to be another Elvis. So, this Pantera isn't just a regular car anymore. It's a canvas that captured a moment in the king's life. This is a testament to how scarcity turns the ordinary into the extraordinary.
