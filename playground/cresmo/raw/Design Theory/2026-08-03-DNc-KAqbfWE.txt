let's move to postor War I Russia to talk about the constructivist movement it was a highly experimental and very modern approach to design and art most importantly these abstract bold designs are actually meant to convey a strong political message after the horrors of World War I Russian constructivists wanted to create social change through Art and Design goal was to create art that represented a fair and just socialist Utopia the constructivists would also use state-of-the-art materials and processes in their work machines factories and mass production were seen as the saviors of humanity once Stalin came into Power the Communist Party felt that there was no room for abstract work in the revolution but why did the state suddenly turn their back on these radical futuristic design movements well here's the thing you got to understand about propaganda all dictators understand the power of Art and Design and they know that creative expression can undermine their regime from that point on all artistic expression in the USSR was purely meant to act as a vehicle for propaganda
