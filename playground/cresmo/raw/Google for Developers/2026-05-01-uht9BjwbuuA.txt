Gemini embedding 2 is our first natively multi-modal embedding model that directly Maps text, images, video, audio, and documents into a single unified embedding space without relying on intermediate text conversions. For developers, this significantly simplifies their architecture. You can pass a raw image or video clip directly to the embedding endpoint, and receive a vector that is immediately comparable to text queries to give you more control. We're using matryoshka representation learning. You can request the default 3072 dimensions, or truncate to 768 to optimize for scale.
