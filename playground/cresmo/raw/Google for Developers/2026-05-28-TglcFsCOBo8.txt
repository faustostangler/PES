Chrome is officially entering the new era of energetic web development, with AI redefining who builds, how we build and what we build for the web. You saw the highlights on the I/O stage, and now I'm going to walk you through the new features and take you on a tour of the interactive demo we had on display at Google, IO and our dev prompt lab. You can explore modern web guidance at work. Level up your expertise by testing your knowledge of performance, UX, accessibility and forms against modern web guidance in this challenge. Powered by DevTools for agents evaluator.

Alternatively, you can switch over to memeo performance, give your website URL, measure its performance with DevTools for agents, and then let the coding agent either praise or roast your results with the Ascii based emoji. Laugh and improve at the same time, modern web guidance and Chrome DevTools for agents are a perfect match. They enable building on a solid foundation and enable rapid runtime evaluation of the generated code. To dive deeper into these tools, make sure to check out more content LinkedIn the comments.
