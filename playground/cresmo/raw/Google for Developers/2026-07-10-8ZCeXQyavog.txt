What's your go to prompting hack to not assume things. Not assume that it knows everything that I have in my mind for now and in the future, or that it remembers everything I said. So be a bit more specific to save my time and my tokens. Ask the LLM to help me with the prompt. So first, I have a session where we go over all the possible angles, what are the things that I definitely do want.

I definitely do not want saving my prompts in M.D. files that I can reference and reuse later. Split the task into smaller chunks, because I've noticed that the agent handles smaller tasks way better than the one big task. It is a brain dump.

Don't overthink. Just, say it like you would say to a coworker.
