ANUDA WEERASINGHE: Hey, everyone. Today we're excited to launch Gemini 3.5 Live Translate, our latest model built for seamless speech-to-speech translation. While it's already been powering experiences in Google Translate, we are now bringing this capability directly to developers via our API. You can integrate real-time translation across 70-plus supported languages for both input and output starting today.

We're going to show you what it can do with two demos. [MUSIC PLAYING]

OK, so for our first demo, we're going to show you a live dubbing experience. Here we're using the API to stream translated audio directly from a tab. Watch as we listen to the Google I/O keynote in Hindi. SPEAKER: What's really incredible is how people are using our AI. GEMINI: [SPEAKING HINDI]

Here, as a speaker, I can create a new session, and I can capture the audio from my microphone. I then get a URL or a QR code for my attendees to scan this on their phone and join the session and start listening in their own preferred language. So let's start maybe with Japanese.
