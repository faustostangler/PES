Scaling multimodal RAG. Here's how Gemini embedding two leverages matryoshka representation learning, or MRL, for massive efficiency. It allows for dynamic truncation, meaning the most critical semantic data is nested right at the beginning of the vector. You can truncate to your specific needs in a single API call. Less storage, same context, and for bulk jobs, you can use the Batch API to process everything at 50% of the cost.

You get significantly lower storage costs while maintaining high level accuracy. Check the cookbook for the code samples.
