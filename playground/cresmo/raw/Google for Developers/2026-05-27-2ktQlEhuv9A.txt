We just wrapped up the developer keynote at Google I/O 2026. You might have seen the announcements on stage, but now let's get you up close with two demos that showcase some of our updates. First, building adaptively for all the devices in your users lives as they transition between phones, foldables, tablets, laptops, automotive displays, and immersive XR environments throughout their day. Android 17 sets a new baseline by making adaptive resizable the standard for apps targeting API level 37, ensuring your app is ready to capture the multi-device users who outspend phone only users by up to 9 times. Jetpack Compose makes it easier to build adaptive experiences with new APIs like grid and Flexbox layouts, navigation, 3D scene decorators, and experimental MediaQuery and styles APIs.

Second, build Android apps faster with Gemini in Android Studio. Here are the highlights. We've got a new project assistant, so with a simple prompt you get the full app implementation, including beautiful UIs that scale across devices. Expanding beyond New project assistant. We've equipped Android Studio with Android expertise via our open source skills and a searchable Android knowledge base.
