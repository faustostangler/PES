Here are three Python projects you should do if you're a beginner learning to code. The first is a habit tracker. Build an app where people can track if they go to the gym or study. You can use the SQL light library to store streaks and habits. And the nice part is you can actually use your own app. Second is a

quiz app where you ask the user multiple choice questions and keep track of their score. You can use tinter to create a nice desktop interface. And this will be a huge jump in your progression because you're actually creating nice UI. Third is an expense tracker where users log in their expense and track totals over time. You can use Python's library mapplot lib to see visualization. Bonus

points if you can integrate an AI chatbot into it to ask it to analyze your spending habits.
