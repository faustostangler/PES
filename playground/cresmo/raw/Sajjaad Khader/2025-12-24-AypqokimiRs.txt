There's one computer science concept that will kill your career if you don't know it, especially with AI coming into power. The concept is called context engineering and it has three main components to it. So, you know how everyone has access to AI? Well, how you use AI and what you can get out of it, context engineering, will determine how long you have a career for. The first component is role specification. Most

people treat AI like it's a generic chatbot. But the best engineers tell it exactly who to be. Be my tutor. Be a Beck and software engineer. This way, you get the correct code optimized for your task at hand. The second component

is context injection. Most people tell AI build me a feature and then blame the model when the output's messy. A bad prompt in gets a bad output out. The best engineers say given these inputs under these constraints, optimize for this outcome. Once the problem is clear, AI will give you the best output faster than anyone else. Third component to the

most important one is task decomposition. Most people ask AI to solve their problem in one shot. Like build me this whole app. The top 1% engineers actually break up their tasks. First design, then logic, then code.

Because AI is better at narrow coding tasks. Hope this helps.
