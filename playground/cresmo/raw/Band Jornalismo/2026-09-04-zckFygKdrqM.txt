Wildfire smoke can travel hundreds of kilometers. Smoke from recent fires in France even reach Germany. And it does more than just pollute the air. Wildfires make climate change worse by releasing huge amount of greenhouse gases. But their smoke makes things even more complicated. Tiny particles in the

smoke can reflect sunlight which can temporarily cool the Earth. Others like black carbon absorb sunlight and warm the atmosphere. And when the smoke rises high enough, it can help create special clouds that can both reflect sunlight and trap heat. So smoke from wildfires is not just a pollution issue.
