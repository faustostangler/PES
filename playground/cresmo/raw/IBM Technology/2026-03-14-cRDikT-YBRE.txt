I guess things are frothy enough in AI world. That Meta decided that it was a good idea to acquire the Moltbook team. I guess the first question is like, why? Why would you acquire it if you were, if you were Meta? They're buying the infrastructure layer of how the future intranets will actually function.

Think of this as the agent social graph versus the human social graph. So I think for 20 years or more, Meta has value was built on the human social graph who you know, what you like, you know etc. and in the agentic era We also need something like an agent graph. So so Moltbook here it acts like as a registry and a directory. If your personal agent needs to hire maybe a specialized travel agent or a legal agent.

It needs a way to find one, and it needs a way to verify its identity and see its reputation like upvotes, downvotes, etc. from other agents. And Meta here, I think it's it's wants to kind of to be the DNS or the "domain name system" for agents. So by owning something a directory like this, it can control the discoverability of every agent on the web. And I think that's really huge.
