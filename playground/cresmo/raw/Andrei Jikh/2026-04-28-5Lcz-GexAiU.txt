The theory is that disrupting Middle Eastern oil flows and cutting off cheap oil from places like Venezuela would hurt China and Russia more than it would hurt the US. So the goal was to starve China of cheap, gold-oil, force them to pay a premium for whatever oil they could find somewhere else, drain their foreign reserves and slow their economy down. Basically more or less the same for Russia, which was already under sanctions. But they would also be squeezed as global energy markets would be disrupted. And the US, with its shale production and its military power, would once again be dominant over the world's energy.
