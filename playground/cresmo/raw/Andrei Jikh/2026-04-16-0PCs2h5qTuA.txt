Imagine Tesla announces a digital wallet. You load it up with dollars, Tesla takes those dollars and invest them into US Treasury bonds earning, let's say, four or five percent. Tesla keeps a little, passes some back to you in the form of rewards, discounts on your next car, maybe charging credits, maybe an actual yield. Right, you're happy because your money's working for you. Tesla's happy because they're earning a spread on their deposits and the US government is happy because someone bought more of their debt. Now multiply that across every major corporation on Earth. You'll get Apple wallet, Amazon wallet, McDonald's wallet, Google Pay with yield, every airline, every retailer, every platform we already use. All of them will become distribution channels for US government debt. All of them holding US Treasuries as the backing for whatever digital asset or rewards program they'll offer you. Now, if that sounds like some crazy theory, this is literally what the legislation moving through Congress right now requires.
