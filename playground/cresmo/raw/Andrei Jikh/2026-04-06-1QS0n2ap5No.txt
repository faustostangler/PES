This graph tracks what's called true interest expense. It's a way of measuring how much of the government's tax revenue is already spoken for before it funds, roads, and schools, and things like that. This number combines social security, Medicare, health spending, and interest on the debt. In February, 2026, true interest expense was 408 billion. That's what the US has spent. With the US made, AK the total tax receipts that same month was 313 billion. That means the government's basic obligations already exceed everything it's made in taxes by about 30%.
