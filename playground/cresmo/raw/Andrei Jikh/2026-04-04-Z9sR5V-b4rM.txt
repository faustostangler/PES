Every other time the Fed printed money, oil was cheap or going down. And what we know is that printing money with cheap oil just inflates asset prices. Printing money with expensive oil inflates everything. Food, energy rent, and every input cost in the economy. And if people can't afford to buy the basic things, that's not good for stocks. That scenario turns into what some economists call a hyperinflationary sovereign debt crisis and stackflation. In this scenario, everything gets hurt, except for, again, potentially cash and gold, and maybe Bitcoin. But none of that is guaranteed. That's arguably the worst possible outcome, and hopefully it doesn't happen.
