Programmable money is different because that money comes with conditions fused into the currency itself. So imagine you get a paycheck and it's only valid for a certain amount of time before it expires. Or it can only be spent within your state. Or it can't be used to buy certain things. Maybe a certain cryptocurrency. Or meat because of a carbon policy or energy is just too expensive. Now, I'm not saying that these specific rules are coming tomorrow. I'm just saying that the technology to do all of this already exists. And the legal framework to deploy it is being built right now.
