The 30-year bond pays about 4% right now, which means a Japanese pension fund or insurance company can now look at a Japanese government bond and say, "Hey, maybe we should put our cash here instead, where we get a guaranteed yield at home in my own currency with no exchange rate risk." Now, that fund holds roughly $230 billion of US Treasuries alone, plus hundreds of billions of dollars in US stocks. And what happened then was the yen went up and their bond interest rates went down, the biggest drop in a month. So, now every Japanese insurance
