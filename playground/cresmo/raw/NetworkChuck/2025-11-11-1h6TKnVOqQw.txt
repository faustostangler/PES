If you didn't know this, your car is pretty stinking vulnerable. Now, this really wasn't an issue until we decided to add 4G and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and all the cool ways we can connect to our car, giving hackers the ability to connect to our cars and hack them. Your car is not just one computer, it's hundreds of computers doing their own specialized things. And all these computers are connected in a network called the [music] CAN bus. Now, here's the glaring problem with the CAN bus.

The CAN bus is a broadcast network. Means when one computer on that network sends a message, that message goes out to every single system on that network. That's bad, but why? [music] Well, if I were to connect myself to that network, I could send messages to every computer on that network, malicious messages.
