[MUSIC PLAYING] SPEAKER: I've been working in animated films, like Pixar's "Inside Out 2," for the last eight years. My name is Connie He, and I'm the director for "Dear Upstairs Neighbors." For this film, the creative challenge was to bridge the gap between art and technology. For the visuals, we really wanted to bring in abstract textures and styles. My father is an artist, and his painting inspired aesthetics we captured on screen.

But hand painting 7,000 frame in such a complex style wasn't feasible. We had to scale the process without losing that human touch. Our team handled the core of this film, then we customized our workflow with AI by training the model with the art and paintings that we created. AI didn't replace the process. It unlocked it.

It added consistency to the living painting look that I always dreamed of. We didn't type this film into existence. We crafted it with a team of 45 people and brought it to life with this new technology. And that's how we made "Dear Upstairs Neighbors."
