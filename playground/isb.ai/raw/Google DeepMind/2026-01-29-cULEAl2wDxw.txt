With Project Genie, you can build worlds based on images you've taken or created. This is helpful if you have an object of your own that you want to build a world around.

For example, you could take a picture of your pet in your apartment. You can upload your photo to Project Genie alongside a detailed description of your environment, and some character descriptions, to define how you move around and interact with the world. These descriptions are helpful to make sure that the environment and character respond in the way you envisioned. Then, click "Create World". And in just a few moments, you can start exploring your living room, from your pet’s point of view.

You could take a picture of a project you’ve been working on, and stage the environment to make it a rich world to play around in.

Or you can bring your artwork to life, expanding its scope and adding in a playable character to explore it in a new way. You can even make a world based on yourself, using Nano Banana to create a portrait in the style of a retro video game. As well as a low poly environment to go with it.

Start creating worlds of your own at labs.google/ProjectGenie
