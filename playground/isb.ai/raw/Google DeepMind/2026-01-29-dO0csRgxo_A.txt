With Project Genie, the worlds you explore can also inspire new ones. Currently, you can build your own worlds or explore a gallery of pre-made ones. You can use these gallery worlds as a jumping off point for making new worlds of your own. For example, if you like this Meadow world but want to explore it with a different character, you can simply modify the sketch with natural language.

From there, you can modify your character and environment to make as many new worlds as you want.

Start creating worlds of your own at labs.google/ProjectGenie
