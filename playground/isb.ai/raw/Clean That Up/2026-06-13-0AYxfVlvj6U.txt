Now you might be wondering Brandon is this job really all that important to do I mean it's behind the fridge no one's ever going to see the dust but the reason you want to do this is when the coils get full of dust into breath it essentially creates like a dust sweater around them and holds heat in place so then your refrigerator has to work so much harder to stay cold.
