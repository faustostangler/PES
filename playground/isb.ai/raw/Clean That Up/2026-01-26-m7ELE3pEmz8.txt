I've walked into a lot of kitchens for customers that are like, I can't figure out where this odor is coming from. And the first thing I always clean is the garbage disposal. Now, the nice thing about this job is it's super simple to do. All you need is just a garbage disposal cleaning pod or tablet. I like to use the ones from Active. You can get them over on Amazon. To do this, just follow the instructions on the box. Many times you just run warm or hot water in the sink for about a minute, then reduce that to a really slow trickle and turn the faucet away from the drain. Then you just throw in the tablet and turn on the garbage disposal and let it do its thing. Now, I will say if you've never done this before or maybe yours is very dirty, it can sometimes be a good idea to add two tablets that way you just have a little extra cleaning power, but this is always my favorite part because it's so satisfying. You see that foam start to come up and many times you can see the grime and things even lift away. Now, once that's done, turn off the garbage disposal and give everything a really good rinse. And it's also good to remember that you don't want food. That's very starchy or fibrous. Going down the garbage disposal because that will create some issues. In fact, I have a plumber friend who always likes to remind me that you should always scrape off your food into the garbage before you put it in the sink. That way you minimize the amount of food that could go down the garbage disposal and potentially cause issues.
