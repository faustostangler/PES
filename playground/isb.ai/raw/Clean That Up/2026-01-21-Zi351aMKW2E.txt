What happens inside your dishwasher? Most dishwashers run in cycles using the same few gallons of water throughout. First is the pre-wash where the water circulates to rinse food off of your dishes. If the sensors in the dishwasher notice that there's still any bits of food on your dishes, the dishwasher will run a more effective cycle giving you a better clean. During the main wash cycle is where the hot water and detergent is released. The enzymes in the dishwasher detergent dissolve food particles and wash them down the dishwasher's drain. This is why you don't need to pre- rinse and instead just scrape the food off your plate. Then it gives a final rinse and a heat try to prevent water spots. And now you know!
