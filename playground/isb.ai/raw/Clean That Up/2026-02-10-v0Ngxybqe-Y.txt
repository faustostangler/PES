If you feel stuck or like you have no motivation to clean whatsoever, you are not alone. I have felt that way myself many times, but something that's really helped me over the last few years is a simple little timer like this. All I do is set the timer to like 10, maybe 15 minutes. And I tell myself all I have to do is clean until the timer goes off. Now some days the timer goes off and I don't want to do anymore cleaning. And that's pretty much all I do for the day. And that's okay. I try to remind myself like 1% a day is better than nothing. But many times what happens is the timer goes off. I kind of got the ball rolling. And before you know it, I'm cleaning all the things I wanted to clean. And I'm starting to knock off all those items on the to do list. And it just helps me kind of spark that motivation to get going and just get off the couch and get moving instead of kind of being crippled by my anxiety of like, oh, I got to do all these things. And many times I just sit there and do nothing at all. So the next time you're feeling a little unmotivated, try a timer, whether it's one of these or your phone, just set it for 5, 10 minutes and see what you can do.
