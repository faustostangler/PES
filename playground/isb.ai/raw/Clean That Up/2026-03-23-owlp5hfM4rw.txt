The next hidden spot on the list is your hood range filter. Now oftentimes these get forgotten because we don't always see them. They can got to be tucked and hidden away at times. But these will get full of grease and grime over time because literally that's their job to pull all that out while you're cooking. And if you don't clean that, that only does it get grimy and sticky. But it can really start to stink. All you need is just a little bit of dish soap or dawn power wash. Spray that down. Let it soak in that soapy water for about 20 minutes. Then give it a gentle scrub and then get it nice and dry before you put it back in. Usually with these mesh ones you can clean them a few times and get some life out of them. And once they get to a certain point where you just feel like they're not cleaning up that well anymore, then I like to replace it. If your hood vent does have a cover like this one here, as you can see it is full of grease and grime. So we also want to clean that too. I like to just use a little bit of dish soap, warm water, and just give it a really good scrub, then rinse, and make sure it's completely dry before you put it back in place. Now the other area that can be a little sneaky too is actually in the hood vent itself. Sometimes grease and grime will develop there, especially if you haven't changed the filter in a long time. So for that, I suggest using just a little degreaser and some damp towels to scrub those areas.
