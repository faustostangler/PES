Cryptocurrency has hung in there since the launch of Bitcoin in 2009. It has become a major player economically and politically. And yet crypto is still in the popular imagination, tainted by its association with the underworld, with Silk Road, the drug trade, bribery, ransomware scams bilking Americans out of $9.3 billion in 2024 alone, according to the FBI. Is that entirely fair?

I mean, yes, many unsavory dark web transactions do use cryptocurrency. But you know what else circulates on the black market? A lot of US dollars. The negative side is really only half the picture. At the end of the day, it's a technology.
