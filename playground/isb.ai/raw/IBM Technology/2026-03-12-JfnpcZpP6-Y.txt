I think there's a genuine accountability gap here. And when you have these agents autonomously publishing some defamatory content and the owner claims it acted independently. Deniability has been woven into the system by design, and that really can't be acceptable. So I think when it comes to ownership of an agent, that really means accountability for its actions. And, the same way organizations are held responsible for the products that they provide to their customers or vendors or suppliers.

And none of this works without a verifiable agent identity. Another aspect, I think is really impactful that could really shift the the tides here is implement like some sort of kill switch because without that infrastructure, AI misuse will scale faster and we can see that it's happening. And that's certainly faster than we can defend against it.
