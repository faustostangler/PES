It's time to stop the back and forth prompting. This year at I/O, we've supercharged your AI coding agents with new AI tooling updates, helping you ship better and faster all before you finish that cup of coffee. First, we're excited to introduce modern web guidance. Think of this as a direct line from Chrome's web expert to your AI agent. Our guidance empowers your agent to build with the latest features and the right fallbacks.

With your baseline target in mind, it helps you drop unnecessary code and third party dependencies, helping you improve both performance and security. How's that for an energy boost.

Next, we're supercharging coding agents with Chrome DevTools for agents. This gives your AI the power to actually control and inspect the browser with access to the runtime of your site, including Console errors and warnings, network requests, and performance traces. Your agent can confidently act instead of guesstimating from your static code. And the best part. You can use both today in anti-gravity or any other coding agent.

Speaking of agents, we are bringing the same agentic power directly into AI assistants in Chrome DevTools. Debugging used to be a slow drip process. Lots of back and forth between multiple tools to understand the full picture. Now with extended capabilities, one prompt provides holistic, concise, and actionable insights to improve the performance of your website. Plus, you can click Show agent walkthrough to understand Gemini's thinking and copy to agent to take all details right into your own agent.

From agents that can finally see the browser to one prompt performance fixes. We're making the daily grind of web development a whole lot easier. We can't wait to see what you build. Thanks for watching and don't forget to stay caffeinated.
