So there are two phases to removing a feature from Chrome deprecation and removal. The deprecation phase is to warn developers that a feature is scheduled for removal in future, and to provide resources to help sites migrate to alternative solutions. Warnings are displayed in Chrome DevTools when deprecated features are used. Deprecation, timelines and other supporting information are published on Chrome platform status in Chrome for developers articles and in blog posts. Now, once a feature has been deprecated and Chrome engineers are confident that usage has dropped to very low levels, Chrome can move towards removing the feature from the browser codebase.
